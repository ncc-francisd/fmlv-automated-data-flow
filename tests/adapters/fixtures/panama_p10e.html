Ford Tourneo Custom Active 232PS | 229BHP 2.5L Petrol Engine

Powerful, smooth, and efficient, the All-New Ford Tourneo Custom Active is equipped with a 232PS | 229BHP 2.5L petrol engine, paired with an advanced automatic gearbox and a generous 63L fuel tank. Designed for confidence and comfort on every journey, it comes with smart features including Keyless Entry & Start, Speed Sign Recognition, Lane Centering with Stop & Go, Driver Alerts, Traction and Torque Vectoring Control, Trailer Sway Control, Electronic Stability Control (ESC), plus LED headlights with static bending lights.

Cruise Control with Intelligent Speed Assist

Intelligent Speed Assist is designed to aid your speed control, enabling you to concentrate on the road. First, a front-facing camera automatically detects the current speed limit. If activated, the system adjusts your maximum speed setting to that limit.

​

Please note It remains the responsibility of the driver to ensure speed limits are adhered to at all times.

Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking and Hill start Assist

The Pre-Collision Assist feature uses camera technology to detect a potential collision with a vehicle or pedestrian directly in front of you during day or night time driving.
