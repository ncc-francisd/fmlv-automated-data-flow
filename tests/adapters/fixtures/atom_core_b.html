Open, sociable and intelligently designed

The Core 600 B features a versatile twin-bench seat layout that creates a bright and welcoming space for relaxing, dining and enjoying time away. With a complete kitchen, private bathroom and everything needed for independent travel, it is an amazing introduction to the ATOM range.

HOME. ANYWHERE.

Designed to drive anywhere, and escape everywhere.

Key Core features include a two-berth layout, twin bench seating, a complete kitchen, a bathroom with shower and toilet. solar power and vehicle tracker, Truma heating and hot water all as standard. All on the reliable Volkswagen Crafter chassis.

Interior

Every centimetre doing something.

Enjoy the drive with a 10” DAB+ media centre with App connect. A wireless charging pad for your mobile phone and a digital cockpit.

The twin benches easily make up into two single berths at the end of the day with minimum effort.

Every ATOM kitchen includes a two burner/sink combination unit with separate oven and grill. There's also a 70ltr compressor fridge with integrated freezer included.

The combined washroom features a basin and shower area, with a 12V electric flush cassette toilet. Complete with chrome taps, a mirror and bathroom window.

With integrated shelf storage, and aircraft style soft close locker doors as standard, you can even choose between two different colours.
