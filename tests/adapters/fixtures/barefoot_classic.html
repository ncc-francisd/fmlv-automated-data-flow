Barefoot Classic

Barefoot Classic has a homely feel with a cream interior and solid oak worktops. A duck egg blue bathroom and grey ‘limed oak’ floor complete this soft and classic look.

Inside the spacious light filled interior, you will find: a fully fitted kitchen with oak worktops, a beautiful bathroom with wooden duckboard, luxurious seating that converts to a king sized bed, and plenty of storage. There are many clever features such as the shower that can be used outside to hose off mucky pups, the wine rack, the under seat storage and the wardrobe that carefully stores the table when it isn’t in use. The Barefoot Caravan is beautifully styled, inside and out, built to the highest standards and lightweight, so that it is easy to get up and Go-Barefoot. 

Most aspects of the Barefoot Caravan are bespoke, from the galvanised steel chassis, to the unique curved body and bathroom. Hand made aluminium hinges, a hand built kitchen, wardrobe and living space make the Barefoot truly exceptional.

Tailor your Barefoot Caravan just for you.

The Barefoot fibreglass monocoque body has been highly acclaimed and will give years of leak-free enjoyment. It is available in a range of colours.  There are many upholstery choices too, and if we don’t have what you’re looking for, please just ask!

Add to that a choice of lighting (orange, warm white or blue), standard grey or coloured side graphics, and not forgetting your choice of colour of Roberts Revival Radio and you have a really special caravan.

FAQs

Why is this model called the Barefoot Classic?

The Barefoot Classic was the first model that we produced 10 years ago. We knew that with its classic styling, oak work tops and high quality fittings it would stand the test of time. After only a few years Barefoot Caravans were being described as “iconic”. Class, style and quality will never go out of fashion – that’s the hallmark of a true “Classic”.

What is so special about the fiberglass Barefoot Caravan body?

The fiberglass caravan body is made from one single piece of fiberglass. It is a bit like an egg – technically known as a monocoque construction. It is an unusual construction and takes a lot of skill. In the US they often call this style of caravan an “egg camper”! The floor is also laminated as it is combined with the body so the whole caravan is watertight. There are no seams or joins in the body that might leak so this makes it very durable and longlasting .

Where did the name Barefoot come from?

Some of the coolest, most laid back people we know are surfers – barefoot surfers. This was the feeling that we felt most suited our cool caravan and the outdoor lifestyle that Barefoot Caravans enable.
