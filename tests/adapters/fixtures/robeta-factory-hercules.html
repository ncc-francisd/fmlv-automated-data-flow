Two dimensions of premium travel · 02

The greatest journeys ask for more than distance. They ask for a place that becomes entirely your own.

Inspired by the calm precision of contemporary yachts, Hercules introduces a new spatial experience to Robeta: a longitudinal electric lift bed, a residential bathroom, a transformable lounge and a kitchen tailored to your rituals.

Floor plan

A private apartment, drawn for the road.

The 6.97-metre layout separates every function without breaking the flow. An open lounge, generous kitchen, separate shower and flexible rear garage form a grand-tourer that never feels improvised.

Private architecture

Made for the journey after the journey.

01 · Sleep

A bed that rises to the occasion

The longitudinal electric lift bed offers a generous 200/185 × 160 cm sleeping space. Raised, it frees the rear garage and its drawers; lowered, it becomes a bedroom framed by ambient light.

01

02 · Lounge

Four seats, one table

Both cab seats swivel towards the bench and table, so the front of Hercules becomes a dining room for four with a view out of every window.

02

03 · Ritual

A kitchen designed around you

Configure the full-height column for display storage, coffee, wine or even a dishwasher, with an optional 140-litre refrigerator. Glassware has its own illuminated rack inside the column.

03

04 · Privacy

A bathroom with room to reset

The separate shower and intelligently divided wet zone give everyday rituals the space and privacy they deserve, with a real washbasin and a window of its own.

04

05 · Transformation

From lounge to cinema

A rotating bench and pop-up television turn the salon from an open social space into a quiet private retreat, and the screen faces the bed for a film before sleep.
