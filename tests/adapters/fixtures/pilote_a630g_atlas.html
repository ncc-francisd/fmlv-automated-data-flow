Compact low profile

A630G ATLAS

Discover the A630G: a compact motorhome, only 6.30 meters long, built on a Ford Transit chassis. Designed for adventurers, it combines a rugged "baroudeur" look, a compact size, and robust performance, offering you comfort and freedom for all your getaways, from the mountains to the wild. It's the ideal partner for unforgettable adventures.

The A630G in detail

This compact and comfortable motorhome handles just like a van. Its go-anywhere size and 165 hp automatic gearbox are perfect for winding roads and small villages. It'll take you wherever your wanderlust calls.

The lounge

Enjoy a comfortable lounge for five, featuring high-density foam face-to-face bench seats that easily convert into driving seats. This relaxing space boasts a dropleaf central table, providing more room to move, and ample high cupboards for easy storage. The interior is bathed in natural light, thanks to the side windows and an opening skydome.

The kitchen

A cleverly designed kitchen where everything is within reach in a perfectly organized space. It features high-quality equipment: a 142 L absorption refrigerator, a 2-burner gas hob, and a sink cover to extend your workspace. Numerous smart storage solutions are also included for your utensils and provisions: drawers and a large pot-and-pan drawer, overhead cupboards, spice racks, and more. As an option, choose the compression refrigerator to reduce your gas consumption, cool more efficiently, and maintain a constant temperature, whatever the conditions!

The bathroom

Enjoy a large, separate bathroom bathed in natural light and ventilation from its skylight. This space is cleverly designed and convertible, thanks to a removable partition that transforms the toilet/washbasin area into a spacious shower.

The bedroom

The dedicated sleeping area features a 140 x 200 cm transverse bed over the garage, complete with a comfortable mattress on a slatted base for ultimate comfort. This bright space, illuminated by windows and reading lights, can be isolated from the rest of the vehicle by a dividing curtain. For additional guests, a 100 x 200 cm electric drop-down bed – a rare feature in a vehicle this compact – and an extra bunk are optionally available in the lounge, accommodating two more people.

The drop-down bed

Despite its compact dimensions, this vehicle will surprise you with its comfortable 100 x 200 cm drop-down bed (optional), discreetly integrated into the lounge area. This is a feat of space optimization and a rare asset in such a compact vehicle. You can also enjoy an additional single berth in the lounge, allowing you to travel the world with four people!

The storage area

With a 1,942-liter volume accessible from both sides and the interior, this space is ideal for storing outdoor furniture (tables, chairs, etc.) or even bikes. It's enough room to bring all your gear for a successful holiday!

The outside

Sporting an adventurous look with its black cab, 16'' wheels, and rugged "baroudeur" decals, you won't go unnoticed on the road. This ultra-modern design, coupled with a compact format, makes it easy to drive and maneuver, all without compromising on interior comfort.
