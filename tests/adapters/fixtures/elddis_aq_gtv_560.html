    Important information regarding the selection of your motorhome

    When buying a motorhome, camper van or urban vehicle (hereafter: motorhome), it is particularly important to choose the right layout and an appealing design. In addition, weight also plays an essential role. Family, friends, optional equipment, accessories and baggage – space must be available for all of these. At the same time, there are legal and technical limits to the configuration and load. Every motorhome is designed for a certain weight, which may not be exceeded during driving. For motorhome buyers, this raises the question: How should I configure my vehicle to accommodate passengers, baggage and accessories according to my needs without the vehicle exceeding this maximum weight? In order to make this decision easier for you, we provide you below with a few tips that are particularly important when selecting your vehicle from our portfolio:

    1. The technically permissible maximum laden mass…
    … is a value set by the manufacturer which the vehicle may not exceed. Elddis specifies a layout-related upper limit for the vehicle, which can vary from layout to layout (e.g. 3,500 kg, 4,400 kg). You can find the corresponding specification for each layout in the technical data.

    2. The mass in running order…
    ... consists – to put it simply – of the basic vehicle with standard equipment plus a standard weight of 75 kg for the driver. It is legally permissible and possible for the mass in running order of your vehicle to deviate from the nominal value stated in the sales documents. The permissible tolerance is ± 5 %. The permissible range in kilograms is given in brackets after the mass in running order. In order to provide you with full transparency regarding possible weight deviations, Elddis weighs each vehicle at the end of the line and informs your trade partner of your vehicle's weighing result for forwarding to you.
    Detailed explanations on the subject of mass in running order can be found in the “Legal Information” section.

    3. The permitted number of seats (including driver)…
    … is determined by the manufacturer in what is referred to as the type-approval procedure. This results in what is referred to as the mass of the passengers. For this, a standard weight of 75 kg per passenger (without driver) is calculated. 
    Detailed explanations on the subject of mass of the passengers can be found in the “Legal Information” section.

    4. The manufacturer-specified mass for optional equipment…
    … is a value set by Elddis per layout for the maximum mass of optional equipment that can be ordered. This limit is intended to ensure that the minimum pay-mass, i.e. the legally prescribed free mass for baggage and retrofitted accessories, is actually available for the vehicle load capacity of the vehicles delivered by Elddis. If, in exceptional cases, the weighing at the end of the line nevertheless shows that the actual load capacity falls short of the minimum pay-mass due to a permissible weight deviation, we will check together with your trade partner and you whether we should, for example, increase the load capacity of the vehicle, reduce the number of seats or remove optional equipment before delivering the vehicle. 
    Detailed explanations on the subject of the effects of the tolerances on the minimum pay-mass and the payload can be found in the “Legal Information” section.

    5. The additional weight of optional equipment and packages...
    ... increases the actual mass of the vehicle (= mass in running order plus selected optional equipment) and reduces the pay-mass. The specified value shows the additional weight compared to the standard equipment of the respective layout. The total weight of the selected packages and optional equipment must not exceed the manufacturer-specified mass for optional equipment.

Equipment Details

Relax and unwind

Relax with your favourite TV Shows - a 22” HD TV, complete with its own mounting bracket, comes as standard, making it easy to unwind with your favourite films and TV shows after a day out!

Bedroom Options

Choose from two rear lounge layouts or three rear bed with garage layouts. The 554, 560 and 563 feature a spacious rear bed with a generous garage beneath. If you prefer the convenience of a bed that’s always ready for a great night’s sleep, while still offering the flexibility to lift it and create additional storage space, these layouts are the perfect choice. Alternatively, the 260 and 460 feature a comfortable rear lounge that makes the most of the living space during the day. When evening arrives, the sofa quickly and easily converts into either a large double bed or two comfortable single beds, giving you the flexibility to sleep the way you prefer.

Thoughtfully Designed

The bathroom in the Autoquest GTV is compact yet still offers you a Thetford toilet, shower, sink and storage. Plus, a 100L fresh water tank combined with the Truma Combi 6 with 10L boiler ensures you always have a great supply of hot running water.

Home Cooking On The Road

The well-equipped kitchen has everything you need to prepare meals with ease, whether you’re making a quick breakfast or cooking a hearty evening meal. The Thetford oven comes as standard, complemented by a practical two-burner gas hob, giving you the flexibility to cook just as you would at home.
