Admiral

THE ULTIMATE

THE ULTIMATE ADMIRAL

 

 

The stylish Admiral van conversion is built on a Fiat chassis and features an automatic 140bhp engine. It offers 2 berths and 4 traveling seats and a generous payload. The layout includes two large single beds or one big double bed at the rear, a central kitchen and washroom, a front dinette and lounge area utilising the driver and passenger seats.

 

This campervan is equipped with a Truma Combi 4 heating system, solar panel, 16” alloy wheels, cruise control and speed limiter, awning, winter pack and a comprehensive specification for year round touring.

 

Storage is plentiful in this campervan, with numerous upper lockers, underfloor and underseat spaces and separate removable lockers under the large rear beds.

 

The Seitz double glazed windows and panoramic skylights at the front and rear give you a superior view from the inside enjoying great natural light. The kitchen is fully equipped with an oven, 2 burner hob, large fridge/freezer, slide out storage and worktop. For your convenience the bathroom is fitted with a fully electric cassette toilet, a shower, sink and storage space for those travel essentials.

 

Whether its short trips or extended stays, the Admiral is a great campervan for all seasons and holidays.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Data & accuracy: The contents of this website are as accurate as possible at the time of going to press, but this information should not be regarded as an infallible guide to current specifications, nor does it constitute an offer for the sale of any particular vehicle. Details of performance, dimensions and weights are subject to minor deviations within manufacturer tolerance limits (max +/- 5%).

The Mass In Running Order (MIRO) - Weight of the Motorhome equipped to the manufacturer’s standard specification including essential habitational equipment. The MIRO is is the mass of the unladen vehicle, including an allowance for the driver (assumed 75 kg) and a 90% full fuel tank. The MRO is calculated with the fresh water tank empty. If you travel with water in the freshwater tank then the User Payload will reduce accordingly.

 

Maximum User Payload includes Personal Effects Payload, conventional load and the Optional Equipment Payload.


Please ensure that you’ve allowed for the masses of all items you intend to carry in the Motorhome.

 

All data is calculated using metric weights and measurements. The imperial figures quoted are conversions thereof and have been rounded to the nearest inch. All dimensions have a tolerance of +/- 25mm. All masses and weights are subject to a tolerance of +/-5% complying with the European Directive 1230/2012.

 

Your Motorhome weights have been calculated with all water tanks empty and we recommend you travel with all water tanks empty.

 

Heights are measured on an unladen vehicle with the aerial in the lowest position.
