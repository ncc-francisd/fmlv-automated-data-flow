Based on the superb MAN TGE, the Altair is the first of a new range. The WildAx Altair comes in two floorplan designs, including rear twin single beds or a convertible rear U-shaped lounge. The most unique feature of the Altair is the all electric habitation area. Cooking, heating, hot water fridge – all powered from mains and 12v.
Each of these floorplans are available in a Sport variety, including an upgraded front grill fitted with LED laser lights, an exclusive front splitter, custom ‘Sport’ graphics, sport 18 inch alloy wheels with all-season tyres and a full leather interior to your colour choice.

Kitchen Features
The Altair is fitted as standard with an all in one lithium leisure battery system which power the 12v systems and through a built in 3kW inverter will power all your cooking and other needs without being plugged into the mains. The system is charged from the 400W solar system whilst in the wild, from the engine via the ‘supercharger’ whilst driving (up to 80 Amp) or from the mains whilst parked up. Cooking is offered via the 2 induction ring hob and microwave. There is a large 150 litre all electric fridge/freezer. Heating and hot water are provided by the Truma 4D, diesel and mains electric powered system.
