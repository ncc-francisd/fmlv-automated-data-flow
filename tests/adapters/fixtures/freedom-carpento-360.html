    Freedom Carpento 360

    From £23,995

    The Freedom Carpento 360 is an ergonomic and aesthetic caravan that can be towed by all types of vehicles with its compact design and lightness. The common features of a Freedom  Carpento are the front panoramic glass structure, large interior volume, very comfortable standing inside, and good fuel economy. 

    Interior

    Bed Layout

    The Carpento 360’s double bed measures 1.9m x 1.3m (6’3″ x 4’3″), comfortably accommodating 2 adults with room to stretch

    Hot Water & Heating

    Included as standard, the Propex hot water system is mains electric powered with a 9 litre capacity.  For warmth there is a thermostatically controlled, 700W mains electric plinth heater located underneath the offside seat base

    Rear Dinette

    The rear dinette quickly transforms from a dining area for 2 in to a 1.9m x 0.7m (6′,3″ x 2′,4″) single bed

    Lounge

    The Carpento 360’s lounge is a sanctuary of light and openness. Two panoramic windows frame sweeping outdoor views, while the skylight above floods the interior with natural sunshine by day and starlight by night. With seating for four it’s the perfect setting to entertain friends in comfort and style

    Bathroom

    Designed with functionality in mind the Carpento 360’s bathroom features a cassette toilet, hand basin and a retractable mixer tap, giving you the convenience of a full bathroom wherever you roam

    Kitchen

    The 360’s compact kitchen is built for convenience without compromising on function. It includes a 2-burner gas hob, a stainless steel sink with mixer tap and a 50-litre 12V compressor fridge, perfect for keeping your food and drinks cold on the go

    The Carpento 360 surprises with its spacious interior – a perfect fit for a young family

    Lightweight Leisure Awards Judge

    Exterior

    AKS3004 Hitch

    Enjoy a smooth towing experience with the Al-Ko AKS3004 stabilising hitch, fitted as standard to all Freedom models. The AKS3004 uses 4 friction pads to support the tow ball which reduces snaking and pitching

    Being an all-in-one design, the AKS3004 requires no more effort to hitch up than a standard hitch.  The built-in safety indicator lets you know that the Carpento 360 is correctly hitched, drastically reducing the chance of it detaching as you are travelling

    Aerodynamic Design

    With their sloping roof line, low weight and curved front edges, our Carpento range are the ideal caravan for electric vehicle owners
