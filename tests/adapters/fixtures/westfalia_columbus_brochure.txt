A timeless journey
Westfalia - freedom on wheels since 1844
For over 180 years, Westfalia has stood for a spirit of adventure, pioneering spirit and a longing for boundless freedom. The big breakthrough came in 1950 with the VW Kombi - a 
real home on wheels. Sofa bed, fold-out table, comfortable bench seat - everything you need to enjoy life on the road to the fullest. What began as a practical companion for 
explorers quickly became an icon: a symbol of travelling without limits and of life in the open air. Today, Westfalia is part of the renowned Rapido Group - and yet remains true 
to itself. With fresh innovative strength and a clear focus on the essentials: Freedom, independence and unforgettable travelling experiences. 
Westfalia - Because every journey is your story.
