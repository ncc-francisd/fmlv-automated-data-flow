Highlights and specifications

Explorer Z EVO is a 636 cm camper van designed for two travellers who want a refined interior, practical storage and enhanced comfort in everyday use. Built on a Fiat Ducato or Peugeot Boxer base vehicle, it offers two travel seats as standard, with an optional third seat available.

The layout includes a comfortable living area, a functional kitchen and a rear sleeping zone with a double bed. The EVO version features a redesigned rear section with a garage lid instead of standard rear doors, creating convenient access to the storage area while giving the vehicle a clean, integrated appearance.

The rear wall is also prepared for the optional installation of a bike rack, including versions with an electric lift for easier loading and unloading of bicycles. Inside, the bathroom is divided into wet and dry zones, improving comfort during longer journeys.

With two berths, a double floor and all-season insulation, Explorer Z EVO is a compact and carefully arranged camper van for comfortable travel throughout the year.
