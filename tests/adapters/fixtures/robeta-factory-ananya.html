The Birth of Ananya

What is a GlamperVan?

A GlamperVan is a campervan built to glamping standards - the mobility of a van joined with glamping-grade comfort, craftsmanship and materials. The category was created by Rok Kordin under the GlamVan brand. Robeta builds and markets Ananya under licence.

We're Rok and Noemi, the restless souls behind Ananya Glampervan. I'm Rok, architect of impossible spaces and father of Ananya, whose hands refuse to surrender until every line and material whispers its own story of possibility. And this is Noemi, vanlife expert and digital dreamweaver who spends most of her life on the road, understanding that freedom isn't a destination on any map—it's the very essence of the journey itself.
‍
Together, we challenged ourselves with one powerful question: What if the life we dream about isn't something to wait for, but something we can build with our own hands today?

Ananya is more than a van. She represents our refusal to be confined by daily routines and expectations.

A vision for those who don't want more stuff, just want more life.

For those who seek freedom not in business cards designed to impress, but in places where cell phones don't work and LinkedIn profiles don't matter.

This is not just another project— but our rebellion against the ordinary. our rage against the dying of the light.

You will call us crazy.
You will say it can't be done.
That we should stick to the safe path.
Play by the rules.

But we chose to write our own story.

We’re not here to compete with what exists – we’re here to create what doesn’t.
‍
Ananya speaks to those rare souls who’ve always felt there should be more. More beauty in the everyday. More freedom in luxury. More possibility in movement.

She isn't just a name - she's a declaration of intent. From ancient Sanskrit comes her truth: "without equal."

In the valleys of Nepal, Ananya takes on an even deeper resonance - "she who brings light to darkness." And in this vessel of dreams and steel, this meaning finds its physical form. Like her namesake, she refuses to accept the ordinary. In a world of small cramped campervans with tiny windows, she stands defiant - panoramic, open, luminous.

AN INTERIOR DEFINED

BY THOUGHTFUL DETAIL

Windows line both sides of the interior, flooding the space with natural light and providing views of changing landscapes during travel. Heated floors extend throughout, delivering consistent warmth underfoot regardless of outside temperatures.

The bathroom features practical microcement walls in a neutral tone, creating waterproof, easy-to-clean surfaces ideal for wet areas. Within this bathroom space, a natural wood trunk serves as the vanity base, its organic form and visible grain supporting a stone basin sink.

This combination of natural elements adds textural contrast to the smooth microcement surroundings. Natural stones also line the shower floor, offering both visual interest and functional foot massage during use. Soft organic curtains in undyed fabrics frame the windows, providing adjustable privacy and light control through simple, maintenance-free materials.

The kitchen area showcases the Corian® Sand Storm counter top, its speckled neutral surface resisting stains and scratches while offering ample food preparation space. This same durable material continues to the dining table positioned near the couch, creating material consistency throughout the living area. The couch provides comfortable seating for relaxation, dining, and workspaces as needed.

Throughout the interior, curved edges on cabinetry, countertops, and the roof line eliminate sharp corners, preventing injuries during travel while creating a visually softer environment. White cabinetry maximizes storage capacity while their handleless design maintains clean lines throughout this compact yet highly functional space.

We began with a simple question:

what if we created a space around how people truly live?

Not the cramped, compromised version of living that’s become somehow acceptable, but real living. The kind where you can host friends for dinner, work with a view of mountains, or simply stretch out with a book while rain taps against the glass windows above and around you.

There’s something almost rebellious about starting with the couch.
(Yes, the couch. Because that’s where life happens, isn’t it?)
‍
It is not just any couch—but one that stretches nearly two meters in both directions, with a depth that invites you to sink in, to stay, to dream. Eighty centimeters deep, because we believe comfort shouldn’t be measured in compromises.

A kitchen that refuses to compromise

Look around at most van kitchens—a tiny counter, a camping stove, maybe a mini-fridge if you’re lucky. We took one look at that standard and decided the story of mobile living needed a different ending.
‍
Our kitchen starts with what actually matters: space to cook real meals. The counter isn’t just bigger than standard van kitchens; it’s bigger than many apartment kitchens. We wrapped it in Corian® Sand Storm, a surface that catches morning light like desert dawn, because some things shouldn’t be compromised. (And yes, it handles everything from rolling pasta to midnight wine spills without keeping score.)

We chose the Bora® Classic gas cooktop (with automatic reignition, because mountain breezes shouldn't interrupt your cooking) and a proper gas oven that brings the aroma of fresh-baked bread to any location you choose. The 130-liter fridge isn't just about storage—it's about carrying the ingredients for a week's worth of culinary possibilities far from the nearest market.
(Because why should adventure mean living on canned beans?)

This isn’t about reinventing the kitchen. It’s about refusing to accept that “mobile” must mean “minimal.” Sometimes luxury isn’t about gold taps and marble counters—it’s about having enough space to chop vegetables while your coffee brews.

Becase not all maps are meant to be followed.
And some stories demand to be written.
Can a space be both open and intimate?

What happens when a bathroom has no boundaries?

Natural stone pebbles beneath your feet tell stories of riverbeds, while micro cement walls rise cool and confident around you. This isn't about building barriers—it's about creating flow. That's why we chose curtains over doors: soft, waterproof fabric that adds privacy while keeping the space alive. (Because privacy doesn't need permanent walls.)

The Elegance 2G Cut toilet brings maritime engineering inland, where every detail speaks of thoughtful design. Its macerator system quietly transforms the practical into the seamless—one button, no ceremony, no compromise. The black water tank handles the rest with the same quiet dignity, because technology should serve, not complicate.

Natural stone, clean lines, flowing fabric. Each element chosen not just for its function, but for how it works together. This isn't about luxury—it's about rethinking what's possible when you stop treating a bathroom like a secret to be hidden away.
(Because who decided bathrooms needed to feel closed off anyway?)

Can a stretch of sky turn a van into a boundless dream?

What Happens When the Sky Becomes Your Ceiling?

Imagine lying beneath a canopy of stars, the night sky stretching endlessly above you, as if the van itself has dissolved into the universe.

This is the vision behind our Skywindow—a daring leap in campervan design that refuses to accept the ordinary. It’s not just about cutting a hole in the roof; it’s about rewriting the rules, pushing boundaries, and searching for a way to bring the outside in like never before.

We’re deep in the prototyping phase right now, wrestling with the possibilities. The Skywindow isn’t a sure thing yet—honestly, we’re still figuring out if we can make it work. But that’s the thrill of it. We’re testing every angle, tweaking materials, and chasing solutions, because “good enough” doesn’t cut it for us.

Of course, it’s tricky.  But that’s what fuels us. Every prototype edges us closer to something that feels both revolutionary and perfectly right.


Will the Skywindow make it into the Glampervan Ananya? We can’t say for sure yet. But we’re not afraid to try, to stretch, to imagine what others might shrug off as impossible. Because that’s the heartbeat of this van: crafting adventures from the edges of what could be.

Numbers That Define Freedom

6+

Days of pure autonomy

6+ Days of pure autonomy through the EcoFlow 10kWh system—the world's first truly integrated 48V solution. We chose it because adventures shouldn't come with a manual: one system, four charging methods, zero complications. While others piece together power solutions, we went plug-and-play. (Because troubleshooting loose wires isn't anyone's idea of a sunset activity.)

Our dual 450W solar panels don't just collect sunlight—they harvest independence. This isn't just about having power; it's about having the right power system. One that quietly transforms sun into possibility, requiring zero electrical engineering degree. Just pure, reliable energy that lets you focus on what matters: where to park for tonight's sunset show.

9,9

Kilograms of fresh laundry

9.9 kg of fresh laundry while off-grid, powered by our 160L water tank. Yes, we doubled the standard campervan capacity because real freedom isn't about counting liters—it's about forgetting you need to.

The tiny wash system quietly tucks away when not in use, but its presence changes everything about extended journeys.

Each cycle a quiet reminder that home isn't an address—it's the feeling of having everything you need, exactly when you need it, while the world whispers stories through your open windows.

2,2 M³
Intelligent wardrobe design

Standing at 2 meters tall and 1,1 meters wide, this isn't just storage—it's a thoughtfully designed space for your wardrobe. Three distinct zones transform organization into elegance: coats hang at full length to stay smooth, trousers rest properly on dedicated rails, and shelves display everything at a glance. After all, isn't the greatest luxury the absence of compromise?

196 kg
The weight we left behind

By choosing PET foam boards over traditional plywood, we saved the weight of two adult passengers (and their weekend bags). That's extra capacity for what truly matters: your adventures, not your furniture. At just one-third the density of plywood, yet offering superior moisture resistance and thermal properties, this isn't just about being lighter—it's about being brilliantly intentional.

8020
The Art of Industrial Precision

Not all aluminum is created equal. This T-slotted system, borrowed from aerospace engineering, ensures everything stays exactly where it should be—even when the road suggests otherwise. From silently cradling your washing machine to providing rock-solid bed support, it's industrial-grade confidence disguised as elegant design. After all, the best engineering is the kind you never have to think about.

160-litre Flat Water Tank:

Our 160-litre flat water tank ensures you have a reliable source of water, no matter how remote your adventure. Its flat design fits snugly under the van, preserving interior space, while its durable construction withstands the bumps and jolts of off-road travel. So, when you're out there, far from civilization, you can rest easy knowing that water is always within reach.

A marine toilet with dimensions of 360 x 370 x 430 mm, designed for tight spaces with angled walls. It uses 0.5-2.7 L per flush, features an integrated macerator system for waste processing, and includes an electronic valve for releasing black water, ensuring efficient flushing and waste management.

MebloJOGI®  Travel F 24 mattress

Tiny wash washer/dryer

Weighing 17 kg, with dimensions of 35 cm depth and 58 cm width, and a 17L drum, it washes up to 3.3 kg and dries up to 1.5 kg. It’s quiet, efficient, and uses onboard hot water, perfect for campervan laundry needs.

Webasto Hybrid 7 Diesel Heater

This heater, with a total heat output of about 7 kW, uses diesel for heating and gas for hot water, ensuring fast and efficient warmth for larger campervans, with flexible underfloor or onboard installation.

Starlink Internet Access

Starlink is a revolutionary satellite internet service by SpaceX, offering high-speed, low-latency connectivity even in remote areas where traditional internet is unavailable. With download speeds from 50 to 200 Mbps and upload speeds from 10 to 20 Mbps, plus latency as low as 20-40 milliseconds, it’s ideal for streaming, mapping, or video calls under the stars.

SureCall Fusion2Go RV Cell Phone Signal Booster

Designed specifically for campervans, this compact, plug-and-play device enhances cell phone reception, ensuring reliable communication and data access in areas with weak signals. It supports all major US carriers, with a high gain of up to 72 dB, covering up to 5,000 sq ft inside your van, perfect for calls, texts, and browsing.

Waterdrop TSA filtration system

This system reduces over 100 contaminants, including 97% of fluorides, with easy 3-minute installation without plumbing. It retains natural minerals, improving water taste, and is cost-effective, certified for quality.
