    Freedom Microlite Sport

    From £14,995 (Model Shown: £15,240, includes Milenco Security Handle)

    Based on the iconic Freedom Microlite body shell which has been in production since 1973, the Freedom Microlite Sport is our lightest and most affordable caravan.  Featuring a 3 Berth layout and a MTPLM of only 750kgs, it’s the easiest way to enjoy caravanning without worrying about towing weights or damp problems.

    Interior

    Front Double Bed

    Using the table and seat cushions the seating area quickly transforms in to a 1.85m x 1.25m (6′ x 4′,1″) double bed

    Rear Dinette

    The rear dinette easily converts in to a 1.85m x 0.5m (6′ x 1′,8″) single bed using the table to bridge the gap between the seats

    Seating

    With a double dinette at the front and a single dinette at the back the Microlite Sport comfortably seats 6 at two separate tables

    Kitchen

    Minimalistic for true adventurers, the Microlite features a 2 burner gas hob, 12V compressor fridge and kitchen tap/sink.  Storage is plentiful with 3 cupboards beneath and shelves at head height

    The Microlite Sport is small with an MTPLM of 750kg and it’s perfectly formed, low and light with a Tardis-like interior

    Lightweight Leisure Awards Judge

    Exterior

    AKS3004

    Enjoy a smooth towing experience with the Al-Ko AKS3004 stabilising hitch, fitted as standard to all Freedom models. The AKS3004 uses 4 friction pads to support the tow ball which reduces snaking and pitching

    Being an all-in-one design, the AKS3004 requires no more effort to hitch up than a standard hitch.  The built-in safety indicator lets you know that the Microlite Sport is correctly hitched, drastically reducing the chance of it detaching as you are travelling

    Pop Top Roof

    The Microlite’s signature pop-top roof design combines sleek aerodynamics with exceptional practicality. When closed, it keeps the overall height to a compact 2.21 metres, perfect for easy storage and effortless towing. Pop it up, and you’ll enjoy a surprising 1.88 metres of generous headroom inside
