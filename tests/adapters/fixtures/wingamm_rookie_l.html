Rookie L

The compact caravan, without compromise

The layout features a rear entrance door, a large toilet compartment with Cassette toilet and a separate shower corner and kitchen block. The interior is welcoming, balanced and careful in the arrangement of the furnishings. The wide and elegant living area front features a U-shaped dinette and a central table that converts into a double bed. Two large windows (one side and one front) frame the dinette.

In front of the central entrance, the kitchen block, elegant, complete with 2-burner hob and sink. The refrigerator is a 96 lt trivalent unit with a freezer compartment. In the queue a broad double bed on one side with capacious overhanging wall units e toilet compartment complete with sink and cassette toilet on the left side.
