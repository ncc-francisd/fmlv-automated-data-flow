Towing advice:
To ensure that your vehicle is suitable for towing a Campod, please refer to your V5C (logbook) document.
On the first inside page, look for the heading "O: Technical permissible maximum towable mass of trailer". Beneath this, you will find the subheading "O.1: braked (kg)".
If the figure displayed next to this matches or exceeds the MTPLM of the Campod model that you are considering, your vehicle is legally suitable for towing.
You will require a tow bar with a 50mm tow ball attachment to hitch to your Campod. You will also need 13-pin Euro towing electrics to power the driving lights and auxiliary electrics.
If you are unsure of any of the above, please contact us for advice by email at sales@campodcaravans.com , or give us a call on 01594 888 596.

With 3 possible configurations for varying uses, Campods are incredibly versatile.
Swapping between the configurations takes a matter of moments.
DINING MODE
Convert the Campod into dining mode by inserting the 2 table legs into their sockets and then attach the tabletops.
​
Comfortably seats 4 adults!
LOUNGE MODE
Using the treble hinged extension flaps beneath the seats, the Campod can be converted into lounge mode, providing a more comfortable sitting position.
​
(Can also be used as 2 single beds!)
SLEEPING MODE
The Campods seating area converts to a spacious double bed using the tabletops and a pair of inserts to form the base, with the backrest cushions used to fill the gap.
About Us
LAURA ASHLEY
CAMPOD CARAVANS
With 70 years of design excellence, Laura Ashley is one of the world's best-loved home and lifestyle brands. Its journey can be traced back to 1953 when Laura and Bernard Ashley began hand-printing fabric at their kitchen table in their tiny flat in Pimlico after being inspired by a Women's Institute exhibition at the V&A museum.
Founded on a rich design heritage and on traditional values of quality and originality, the Laura Ashley brand has been nurtured to become synonymous with beauty and design. The Laura Ashley style is constantly evolving to remain distinctive and inspirational, evoking the alluring beauty of the British countryside and a timeless mood of peace and serenity.
After nearly 10 years of success in the food trailer market, business partners Tom & James embarked on a new venture to create an innovative lightweight leisure vehicle with a focus on practicality and exceptional build quality.
​
Campod Caravans boast a number of unique and ingenious features that set them apart from other leisure vehicles; wide, side-opening hatches with integrated windows, a recessed footwell providing full standing height while retaining a low overall profile, a large double glazed arched window to allow an abundance of natural light into the interior and an aerodynamic, lightweight fibreglass shell to provide a seamless towing experience.
