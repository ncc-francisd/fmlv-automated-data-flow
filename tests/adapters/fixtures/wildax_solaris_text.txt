The WildAx Solaris offers a very unique layout, giving a full family of four space to eat, sleep and lounge – all in a 6 metre van! The Solaris is an award winning four berth layout with an extremely clever design. The Solaris offers four travel seats and four adult sized beds, a large kitchen and washroom. This is all done whilst still giving a feeling of space, with most owners of the Solaris commenting that they are surprised at the feeling of openness in a motorhome with so many features. The WildAx Solaris XL is one of our most popular motorhome models, with over four awards spanning since 2016!
