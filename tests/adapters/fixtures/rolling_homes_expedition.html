Expedition

The Expedition camper van has a robust interior that can be customised and transformed into your dream van. All campers are beautifully finished and offer optional soft furnishings to complete the look.

The new Expedition is designed to go anywhere. There will be no stopping your adventures with this rugged all terrain vehicle.

This camper has all the equipment and comforts of our whole Rolling Homes range has, but this one has a twist. This Expedition camper will allow you to go anywhere you want. With extra underbody protection, diff locks, 4 motion and many more options, this camper really makes your adventures unstoppable.

The Expedition is one of our most popular campervans, it comes fitted with the traditional side kitchen as standard and includes a fold-down, double RIB bed. It’s finished to the highest standard with luxurious, handmade furniture and is suitable for up to four people. There are lots of elements that make this camper so popular, but there are also many additional extras you can choose from to make sure it is the perfect campervan for you.

The Expedition is a fantastic camper that you can inject with the personality to suit you and your needs, there are many great features to this van that we know you, like many others, will truly love.

Motorhome Qualifed Converters

Motorhome and campervan converters have to meet Volkswagon’s rigorous high standards to gain their place on the Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles Motorhome Qualification Scheme. This includes having our vehicles meet legal type approval standards allowing first registration as a campervan or motorhome, not as a panel van. We must also have gained approval from the National Caravan Council (NCC) who govern habitation standards and safety of our converted campervan.

Volkswagen also insist that converters match the term of our base warranty too.
