Engineered for outdoor enthusiasts

ACTIVE EDITION

The Active Edition is designed to support your most dynamic outdoor pursuits, offering a robust and adaptable solution for transporting your gear with ease. Every feature ensures reliability and convenience, letting you focus on the journey ahead.

‍

  • Custom black aluminum ramp ensuring effortless loading and peace of mind.
  • Heavy-duty guides capable of holding up to 200kg for reliable gear transportation.
  • Railing locking system with a rear extension for easy loading, even outside the van.  
  • Adaptable ramp design with versatile round and oval holes for personalized use.
  • Removable ramp to transform the rear compartment into a seamless, spacious area when not in use.
  • 700mm passage for easy access and retrofitting options for MY2025 models with standard layouts.

Ramp Dimensions:

  • L4: 1620mm x 620mm x 50mm
  • L3: 1270mm x 620mm x 50mm
  • L2: 1070mm x 620mm x 50mm
Available on: Apollo, Ares, Helios

Designed for seamless family adventures

FAMILY EDITION

The Family Edition redefines family travel, offering innovative features that prioritize comfort, functionality, and togetherness.

‍

Whether it's providing parents with a private retreat, creating cozy sleeping areas for children, or maximizing space for play and relaxation, every detail is designed with families in mind.

‍

  • Lifting electric bed providing additional sleeping space for the whole family.  
  • Lounge area doubling as a play zone or additional family space.  
  • Lowered furniture positioned 350mm above floor level for enhanced accessibility.
  • Aluminum folding bench with LATOFLEX and 70mm cushions for optimal seating height of 470mm.
  • Lowering table system enabling smooth transitions to a comfortable lower bed.
  • Thinner lifting bed providing effortless bedtime routines and 1.1m seated headroom.  
Available on: Apollo, Ares, Helios

Engineered for outdoor enthusiasts

FOR TWO EDITION

FOR TWO EDITION

The For Two Edition reimagines travel for couples by creating a flexible, serene environment that blends freedom with luxury. Tailored for two passengers, the new layout centers around a multifunctional table that transforms your space into a mobile office, a dining area for two, or a dual workstation—whatever the moment requires.

‍

Instead of a front bench seat, this edition includes:

- Multifunctional table for two that quickly converts from dining to a comfortable mobile office for paired work.

- Integrated device charging (for phones) so power is always within reach.

- Dedicated bottle/cup holder to keep essentials secured while driving or working.

- Smart storage drawer/compartment for cables, notebooks, and daily accessories.

- Adaptable layout that opens the passage and increases space, comfort, and privacy for two.

‍

Available on: Apollo, Helios, Kronos, Ares
