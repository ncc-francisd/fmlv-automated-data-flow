Mink-S

Download Brochure

The original Mink Camper

The MINK-S is the original Mink Camper, designed in Iceland to make the intimacy of camping effortless and easy through thoughtful details. Its lightweight and aerodynamic design makes it an ideal travel companion for most cars.

MINK
Awnings

Shelter From The Elements.

Designed for wild adventurers and nature enthusiasts, the MINK camper offers a cozy retreat after a day of exploration. Forget wrestling with tents; instead, indulge in essential comforts. Enhance your outdoor experience with our versatile awnings, effortlessly positioned at the back or side of the MINK.

Expand your sleeping accommodations and elevate your camping adventures with MINK’s thoughtfully crafted awning solutions.

Standard Equipment

Interior

Small on the outside but spacious on the inside with a large panorama skylight and round windows on both sides. The juicy queen size luxury mattress ensures a good nights sleep while being the perfect spot to stargaze. Additionally, a canvas bunk bed for a young child, doubles as storage space. Cuddle up and enjoy the wild like never before.

  • Queen size bed (140 × 200 cm)
  • Luxury mattress (memory foam)
  • Canvas bunk bed (140 × 50 cm)
  • Canvas blinds for all windows
  • LED ceiling mood lighting
  • 4 USB ports above the headrest
  • Felt side pockets (one on each wall)
  • Salvador upholstered backrest
  • Luxury mattress (1400 × 2000 mm)
  • Canvas bunk bed (1400 × 500 mm)
  • Large panorama skylight
  • 2× large openable side windows
  • Canvas curtains on all windows
  • 4× USB plugs (from onboard battery)
  • 1× 230V plug (requires mains power)
  • Battery monitor
  • 2× MINK storage pockets
  • 2× side wall integrated static ventilation
  • 2× MINK mosquito nets (for side doors)
Exterior
  • Solid shell structure in UV-protected ABS plastic
  • 20 mm high-thermal insulation
  • 2× front and rear rugged maneuvering handles
  • AL-KO chassis (reinforced galvanised steel)
  • AL-KO chassis with mechanical handbrake
  • AL-KO shock absorbers
  • 13-pin electric trailer plug (charges battery if supported by car)
  • 70 Ah AGM battery
  • 230 V Schuko plug for battery charging and running 230 V appliances
  • DEFA Multi-Charger 1205 Flex
  • Webasto diesel heater (12 V)
  • 105 W solar panel
Kitchen

The back of the MINK Camper opens up to reveal a well organised and illuminated cooking area, with plenty of storage space. The counter top accommodates a built-in illuminated ice chest. You'll also find a 12v plug to power smaller gadgets and 230v plug to use when the Mink is connected to electricity. Find your perfect spot and prepare the next inspiration on the gas stove.

  • Food storage – plenty of space to store your food, drinks, and utensils in a safe space
  • Illuminated ice chest – 36 L, keeps food and drinks cold for days
  • LED lighting – guides the way in the kitchen on dark nights
  • 12 V plug – connect smaller appliances or charge your phone
  • 230 V plug – requires connection to mains power for extended battery life
  • 3× large storage boxes
  • 3× small storage boxes
  • 2× felt storage boxes
  • 1× 12 V plug (from onboard battery)
  • 1× 230 V plug (needs mains power)
  • Illuminated ice chest, 36 L
  • Storage area under counter
  • 1× MINK chopping board
  • Gas stove
