Our compact luxurious Motorhomes

Since 1977 we have always thought about camper as a compact, slender, easy to handle, safe vehicle for all curious travelers and their desire to travel the less traveled roads and reach both cities and the wonderful and unspoiled places of our planet.

For these objectives we immediately chose the semi-integrated camper formula, excluding the solutions with attic, which by sailing, reduce the vehicle's stability on the road, reduce safety and increase consumption.
