Overview

The luxury Bourton delivers a spacious no compromise front lounge end kitchen model. Its huge lounge quickly converts at night to provide a choice between full-length singles or a decadent double bed whilst the Mercedes chassis is renowned for its driving comfort and the optional fully automatic gearbox makes it the perfect base vehicle for a luxuriously specified flagship motorhome range.

The MRO is is the mass of the unladen vehicle, including an allowance for the driver (assumed 75 kg) and a 90% full fuel tank. The MRO is calculated with the fresh water tank empty. If you travel with water in the freshwater tank then the User Payload will reduce accordingly.

The optional automatic gearbox on the Mercedes chassis is included in the Premium pack. The complete pack will increase the MRO by 35 kg and therefore reducing the user payload by the same amount. All masses and weights are subject to a tolerance of +/-5% complying with the European Directive 1230/2012. All motorhomes in this brochure conform to the European Standards for Motorhomes: EN1646 -1, EN 721, EN1949 and EN 1648-2. They have also been granted European Whole Vehicle Type Approval to EC Directive 2007/46. We would strongly advise against modifying vehicles in any way that could affect the validity of these Standards or Directives.

The Maximum User Payload includes:
Conventional load, is the allowance for passengers
Essential habitation equipment includes items and/or fluids required for safe and proper functioning of the habitation equipment.Optional equipment is items available from the manufacturer over and above the standard specification.
Personal effects, items not effected by the above.

When optional equipment is fitted to the Fiamma mounting rails on the rear of this motorhome, the combined maximum allowable load inclusive of the rack, is limited to a maximum of 50kg. The owner is responsible for ensuring this weight restriction is not exceeded, as it will invalidate your warranty if damage occurs from overloading.

Discover Your Home Comforts at a Glance

Explore the standout internal features of your Auto-Sleepers Mercedes-Benz Motorhome. Fuel your adventures in a fully equipped kitchen, Dream Big to Explore more after a restful nights sleep, and refresh on the go with a multifunctional washroom.

Dual Fuel Hob

Kitchens include a flexible dual-fuel hob with separate oven and grill.

Microwave Oven

All Coachbuilts feature a 230v Dometic microwave oven for convenient cooking on tour.

Fridge-Freezer

Keep your food fresh with the large refrigerator with freezer compartment

LED Interior Lighting

Create the right interior atmosphere with bright, energy ef?cient LED lights. Including easily positionable reading spotlights.

Durable Upholstery 

SOur fabrics have passed the Martindale rub test over 100,000 making them more durable with enhanced longevity. Find Out More.

Your Personal Retreat

Retiring to bed couldn’t be simpler. Slide out the bedframe and premium quality Reflex™ foam cushions to double up as a comfortable mattress. The Burford models features the ultimate in luxury with fixed rear beds with memory foam mattress.

Clever Storage Solutions

Thoughtfully designed storage solutions let you make the most of your payload, so you never have to leave behind the essentials—or those little extras that make your adventures even better.

Refined Showering Experience

Luxury extends beyond the living space, with every washroom carefully designed for both practicality and comfort. Depending on the model, washrooms feature either an innovative space-saving shower pod or a separate shower cubicle, complete with an electric flush cassette toilet and a separate header tank for convenience.

Metalarte habitation door with sliding flyscreen door 

Auto-Sleeper Coachbuilts are fitted with a safe and secure exterior habitation door featuring single key locking. For added comfort and hygiene, these doors are also fitted with a useful pleated-mesh flyscreen to help keep annoying pests out of your living area.

USB & USB-C Sockets

Stay powered up wherever you go with USB and USB-C charging points strategically placed throughout the motorhome habitation areas.

Dine Al’Fresco!

Feel like making the most of some glorious weather? All Auto-Sleeper models feature external BBQ points for quick and easy outdoor dining.

Cocktail Cabinet

Complete with crystal wine glasses as standard.
