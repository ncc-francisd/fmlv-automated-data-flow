The Aurora and Aurora Leisure are clever layouts which sleep and travel up to three. Much of the WildAx Aurora’s popularity comes from the versatile design that allows two separate areas in the motorhome. The difference between the two models is simply that the Aurora has a large ‘fixed’ bed that will fold completely up and out of the way to allow access to the huge storage area underneath. The Aurora is unique in the way that under the bed becomes completely empty, giving more space for storage. The Aurora Leisure differs only in the bed area – the ‘Leisure’ rear end gives a lounge style seating area that quickly and easily converts into a double bed at night. Both seat frames have easy access storage under and a robust metal sliding mechanism that allows the double bed to be put out in far less than a minute.
Looking for something a bit larger? Both the standard Aurora and Leisure models can be found in an XL variant, giving additional space, storage and length! These XL models also benefit from a full height fridge/freezer and a microwave in the kitchen.
