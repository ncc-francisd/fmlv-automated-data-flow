ORA F Line

5.99m (19'8")

Click Here to Spec Up Your ORA F-Line
2 Berth
2 Travel Seats

The ORA F-Line based on the Iconic Ford Transit Limited Edition. At just under 6m with an open plan front lounge that can be used as two single beds or into a Super king size. The front seats swivel to add another seating area and is the ultimate social space for entertaining. It features high-end amenities like a fully equipped kitchen, a stylish living area, a comfortable bed, and a well-designed bathroom, making it perfect for long road trips or weekend getaways

Included in the Limited Edition: 170bhp engine, 16″ Alloy Wheels, 12″ touch screen with navigation,  Apple car play, digital rear view mirror to name a few. The colour of the vehicle is Silver.

Take a Closer Look

KITCHEN AREA

The Kitchen area has a full oven, grill, large fridge freezer, lots of handy storage and a fold down work surface. These features make this kitchen extremely versitile.

FRONT LOUNGE

The lounge area is designed to be both inviting and versatile, offering a bright, open space to relax or socialise. Twin bench sofas provide comfortable seating during the day, enhanced by plush cushions and large side windows that flood the interior with natural light.

DINING

The swivelling cab seats and central table easily transform the area into a sociable dining space, while the clean lines, soft upholstery, and warm overhead lighting create a calm, premium atmosphere. It’s a space that feels just as comfortable for quiet evenings as it does for entertaining on the road.

WASHROOM

The large rear washroom is fully equipped with dropdown basin, mixer shower and a handy bathroom cabinet. 

BLINDS 

Throughout the vehicle we fit Remis blinds including the cab. Visually impressive with its surrounding frame and pleated material to help you get a better nights sleep. This prevents others from looking in and protects the cab from sunlight. 

REAR SPACE

At the rear is an external door from the washroom, great for muddy paws and boots to be washed off before entering the lounge space. There is rear boot storage and space to hang chairs on the rear doors.

LED STRIP LIGHTING & UPPER LOCKERS

Throughout the vehicle is LED strip lights to create a cosy ambiance. The upper internal lockers provide great extra storage.

USB / USB-C 

The lounge area is equipped with USB/ USB-C points for easy access

WARDROBE SPACE 

The ORA offers large wardrobe storage space and a hanging rail for clothes in the rear washroom.
