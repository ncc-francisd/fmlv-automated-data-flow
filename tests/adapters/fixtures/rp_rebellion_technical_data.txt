RP Innovation and Technology
At RP, innovation isn’t about technology for its own 
sake. It’s about solving problems, improving the 
owner experience and finding better ways to build.
That thinking has shaped some of the Rebellion’s 
most distinctive features. When Mercedes-Benz 
stopped producing the super-high roof previously 
used, RP designed its own — increasing overhead 
storage, allowing the awning to sit more neatly 
against the body and incorporating a front spoiler to 
improve airflow.
The Rebellion’s elevated rear lounge is another 
defining feature. Raising the living and sleeping 
space creates a garage below, accessed through RP’s 
own up-and-over rear door, while the top-opening 
rear panel gives the lounge a light, open feel and 
elevated view.

Award-Winning Design — Year After Year
That commitment to thoughtful design continues to 
earn industry recognition.
In December 2025, the Rebellion 2 AWD Pro was 
named Best 4x4 Campervan at the Out & About 
Campervan Awards, recognising its design, versatility 
and capability.
Hot on the heels of that success, in February 2026 
the Rebellion 2 won the Caravan and Motorhome 
Club Award for Any All Wheel Drive Van Conversion 
over 6.5 metres.
These latest awards add to a strong history of 
recognition for RP, including the Special Edition 
Rebel Raid AWD in 2025, Rebel AWD in 2024 and 
Rebellion AWD in 2023, alongside earlier category 
wins in 2022 and 2020.
For us, the awards are welcome recognition — but 
the real goal remains the same: to keep refining 
every RP around the people who actually use them.

RP has also embraced modern waterless sanitation, 
with both the Clesana C1 and OGO composting

RP Smart Assist brings vehicle information and 
control together through our own interface on 
an included iPad mini, while Victron 24V electrics 
provide a foundation that can expand to our 
Advanced Power Management system for serious 
off-grid independence.
Alongside these headline innovations are countless 
smaller details — bespoke lightweight panels, 
3D-printed components, custom fibreglass parts and 
solutions developed in-house for the Rebellion.
