A-Class

G690GJ EXPRESSION

The Pilote G690GJ motorhome offers cleverly designed space and smooth handling, making it the ideal companion for unparalleled adventures. Explore new horizons and be captivated by the spirit of adventure with peace of mind. With this A-Class Pilote motorhome, experience memorable getaways and discover the world with ease.

The G690GJ in detail

Stylish and compact (7.07 m), the G690GJ is a perfect compromise. Each space is optimised to combine style and practicality. This A-Class offers ease of movement between the lounge and bedroom, which are entirely separate spaces.

The lounge

This all-in-one has excellent circulation from the living room to the bedroom and a real day/night partition. The living room has 5 seats for meals. It has two L-shaped bench seats and a modular table that makes it easy to move around. Its large windows and panoramic skylight make it a pleasant, bright living space.

The kitchen

Beautiful, well-equipped kitchen on the right with a 2-burner hob, a compression refrigerator, comfortable work surface, numerous drawers and storage units, 230V, 12V and double USB sockets, large bay window and skylight opening to let in plenty of light. Opt for non-slip mats in your drawers: they secure your dishes and provisions while reducing vibration noise during your travels.

The bathroom

Despite coming in at less than 7.10 metres, the G690GJ features a separate shower and bathroom.

The bedroom

The twin beds in the G690GJ can be adapted to suit your needs: two separate single beds or a 170 x 205 cm king-size bed, thanks to the extra sleeping space between the 2 beds (standard). A "low bed" version is also available, allowing you to get into bed easily, without using any ladder. You'll also find storage space and wardrobes under the beds. As an option, choose custom-made fitted sheets, perfectly tailored to the mattresses for maximum time and energy savings.

The storage area

At the rear of the vehicle, the large walk-through hold is lowered to make loading and unloading easier. It's insulated, so you can store your belongings without risk. Thanks to the raised twin beds above, the storage area boasts excellent height, ideal for transporting tall and bulky items. The doors on each side are fitted with compression locks on double seals, ensuring water and air tightness.

The outside

You will love its modern new front and dynamic new rear design. The easy-access gas locker, redesigned for a 2.75L bottle, allows you to maximize your storage space! You can also enjoy a standard external gas socket for outdoor cooking. The 15 cm double floor ensures excellent thermal comfort while offering extra storage compartments. The insulated storage area keeps your belongings safe and remains accessible from the inside thanks to its pass-through design.
