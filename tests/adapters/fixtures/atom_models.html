BIRTH OF A LEGEND

Atom Core

The ATOM Core is the birth of the range, but there is nothing basic about it. Created to make modern campervan ownership more accessible, Core combines a Volkswagen Crafter base vehicle with a complete kitchen, private bathroom, solar power, comfortable living space and the essential technology you need for life on the road.

MORE ADVENTURES

Atom Element

The ATOM Element takes the experience further with an enhanced specification designed for a more striking appearance and greater independence. A powerful 340Ah lithium leisure battery, 3000W inverter and integrated solar system provide increased off-grid capability. An integrated roof rack, awning and ladder add adventure-ready practicality, while features including alloy wheels, motorhome Wi-Fi, a reversing camera and pleated cab blinds complete the package.

The Atom difference

Handbuilt for the way you actually live.

A complete campervan from day one

Every ATOM includes everything you need to travel comfortably, as standard. A complete kitchen with hob, sink, oven, grill and fridge. Private bathroom with shower and toilet. And Truma heating and hot water system.

Purpose built, not converted.

The ATOM is not a campervan conversion, it’s a van engineered as a camper from the first sketch. Purpose-designed storage, insulation, and living systems that actually work for life on the road.

Fully configurable

You can choose from 10 exterior colours, multiple interior palettes, and various options to personalise your ATOM. Built for people who value their individuality and taste. Exactly as you want it.

Built to last
