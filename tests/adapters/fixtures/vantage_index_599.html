FAQ's

How much do motorhomes cost?

Each of our motorhomes are manufactured to offer quality without compromise with several bespoke options and accessories. As a result, the overall price can depend on the final design you choose. Contact us today to speak with an expert and find out more.

Can I buy my motorhome through part exchange?

Yes, we offer the opportunity to buy any Vantage motorhome through part exchange. Once you have selected your ideal motorhome, you can exchange your current motorhome, caravan or car, regardless of make or model against it.

Do you stock campervans?

Yes, we do! We expertly convert and manufacture our campervans at our modern facility in Leeds, so quality is assured in every model. Explore our range today.

5.99M TEO

The TEO has been carefully designed with the ever-popular RIO in mind, ideal for those who want a smaller footprint without compromising on style or comfort. The van is 3 berths with 4 belted seats perfect for day trips or long-haul getaways.

The front dinette easily seats four, with the driver and passenger seats swivelling to create a generous and welcoming dining space. At the rear, the TEO features a spacious wrap-around U-shaped lounge, complete with USB sockets on either side, overhead locker storage with a sleek new door design, and a dedicated TV and shelf area. Come night-time, the lounge easily converts into a luxurious double bed giving you the flexibility to suit your travel style.

The bathroom has been upgraded with stylish textured marble-effect wet wall panels, creating a modern, high-quality finish. A practical vanity unit with mirror offers ample storage for toiletries, while the well-appointed shower, toilet, and sink provide all the comforts of home.
