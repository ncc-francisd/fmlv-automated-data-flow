Panel van 54G

From £58,900

The 54G is the smallest panel van in our range. At 5.41 m in length, it’s ideal for solo travel or for two people. Easy to drive and maneuver in all conditions, it also features a very comfortable interior layout, including a rear garage bed, a fully equipped kitchen, a bathroom, and a lounge area.

French manufacturing

Features designed for your peace of mind

All our panel vans focus on driving comfort, with a 140 hp engine and automatic transmission. The 7” Media Pack (Pioneer, CarPlay/Android Auto) and the rearview camera make every journey easier for a stress-free experience.
On board, the Truma® Combi D4 system, a 100 W solar panel, and the awning ensure your autonomy. Inside, the bench seat is equipped with the ISOFIX system and headrests, offering a modern and perfectly designed interior.

The lounge

The lounge welcomes you around a table with a swivel extension. The cab seats are swivel and height-adjustable. You’ll also benefit from clever storage spaces under the bench seat in the raised platform, a large overhead cupboard, as well as a USB port and a 230V socket.

The bedroom

Sleeping area: uncompromising comfort in the 54G.
The 54G Joa bedroom has everything of a larger model: a comfort mattress (133 × 190 cm) on a slatted base and an ultra-comfortable headboard. For your evening reading, enjoy two reading lights with integrated USB ports. For storage, the four overhead cupboards easily accommodate all your belongings.

The kitchen

An accessible kitchen: cook freely with a modular worktop (sink cover) and a two-burner hob. The real highlight? A clever 95L compressor fridge with dual access, from both inside and outside!
For storage, everything is included: drawers, a pots-and-pans drawer, overhead cupboards, and a spice rack for perfect organisation.

The bathroom

In the bathroom, you can store all your belongings in an overhead cupboard. A smart layout designed to offer you a functional and practical space.
