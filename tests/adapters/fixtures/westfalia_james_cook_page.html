Westfalia James Cook

Make that dream of life on the road a reality with the Westfalia James Cook. Whatever the time of year, you can head off on your travels knowing that you’ve got comfort and convenience neatly wrapped up in this beauty of a campervan. This is a home from home, with dual zone warm water diesel heating and a winter pack with underfloor heating and a heated shower tray; a bed in the main zone and another in the pop-up roof means there’s snooze space for 4, making this a superb choice for families and couples, a large bathroom means you’re not getting under each other’s feet in the morning.

The well-equipped kitchen block has been carefully designed for practicality and cookability; the 90l compressor fridge is accessible from the outside and there’s a 100l freshwater capacity. The spacious modular cargo hold gives you several storage options. But that’s not all; this innovative vehicle is packed with handy systems like a user-friendly electronic control unit, assistance monitors and an offroad optic package. In this handsome vehicle, you’ll be roving in style, with all the onboard luxury and functionality you could wish for.
