ROOKIE 3.5

Our Rookie fiberglass monocoque mini-caravan is instantly recognizable among compact caravans thanks to its rounded, harmonious lines, typical of Wingamm! Small, lightweight, easy to handle on the outside, spacious and functional on the inside; designed and engineered for touring tourists.

Rookie: the caravan holiday

from another perspective

Our mini-caravan with monocoque body in fiberglass Rookie is immediately recognizable, in the panorama of compact caravans, for its rounded, harmonious line, typically Wingamm! Small, light, easy to handle outside, spacious and functional inside; conceived and designed for traveling tourists.

The distinctive character of the Wingamm Rookie monocoque is noticeable at first glance! Lthe only mini-caravan in the world with monocoque fiberglass bodywork without joints and therefore 100% waterproof, excellently insulated with polyurethane foam, completely free of metal parts and therefore without thermal bridges and without the possibility of corrosion.

Rookie Cross

Adventurously comfortable

Ultra-compact (measuring 3.60 meters like Rookie 3.5), but with a decidedly more "adventure" look starting from the external equipment.

The internal lay-out is similar to the Rookie 3.5: rear entrance door, large toilet compartment with Cassette toilet and separate shower corner, kitchen block with sink, 2-burner hob and 70lt trivalent refrigerator, double-glazed windows that can be opened all with blackout and mosquito net.

The double dinette (convertible into a double bed) has been enriched with one cushions made entirely of eco-leather, resistant and easy to manage, in olive green color. The lighting is LED.
