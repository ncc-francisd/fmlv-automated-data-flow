ABOUT OUR CUSTOM FORD TRANSIT CAMPER VAN

 

Our handcrafted, versatile camper vans are perfect for those who value quality. Our Ford Transit Custom Camper Van is fully equipped with a brand new Vantage R Design Styling Pack, and a wide range of exterior enhancements.

Our Vantage R Ford Transit Custom Camper Vans come with a plethora of precisely engineered upgrades. To name a few, the Ford Transit Custom Camper Van includes our full exterior enhancement kit, alloy wheels, the Reimo Rock’n’Roll seat/bed, and most excitingly, the Reimo pop-top roof! The Vantage R  Ford Transit Camper Van isn’t just your basic means of travel; it’s purpose is to create an expedition in style and luxury.

Owning a Vantage campervan comes with a wide variety of benefits. Each vehicle is hand-converted at our very own factory in Leeds with the utmost attention to detail. Whether you need a functional vehicle for daily use or a perfect getaway campervan for unrestricted exploration, our team at Vantage Motorhomes can provide you with the ideal solution. Contact our team today to get started, or book your very own factory tour! There is no better time to start exploring in style.
