Low-profile

P720U EVIDENCE

With the Evidence Edition, discover vehicles designed to make life on board easier, while ensuring comfort and performance. Low-profile motorhomes are distinguished by their elegance and agility on the road, offering travellers the perfect balance between comfort and manoeuvrability for stress-free escapades.

The P720U Evidence in detail

Generous and welcoming, the new P720U, low -profile with U-shaped seating area, offers you direct contact with your surroundings. View of the vineyards or view of the wild side, you will appreciate the opportunity to connect with the outside. Well installed in the large U-shaped living room, you will welcome your friends in your new second home Friendly, comfortable and open to the world and the countryside.

The lounge

Comfortably installed in the XXL lounge, you can enjoy the panoramic view and the surrounding countryside through the 3 large windows. The convivial living room can accommodate up to 7 people, so you can enjoy a nice siesta without even having to turn down the bed. Multi-purpose: if you want to take advantage of a day's teleworking in the countryside, the large table is perfect for your computer and a steaming cup of tea. The wide table can be folded away and the sliding top makes it easy to move around. The numerous LED lights create a warm atmosphere in the evening. The hinged stand can accommodate a 32’ TV.

The kitchen

You'll love cooking up a storm in this light-filled kitchen. Fitted with lots of lighting, it has a large, highly functional worktop, plenty of storage space, a compression fridge and an extractor hood (optional).

The bathroom

Cleverly positioned between the cabin and the living room, you'll love the spaciousness of this bathroom. The large separate shower gives you the space you need to relax. The heat is evenly distributed, so you can enjoy it to the full in complete privacy.

The wardrobe

The walk-in wardrobe, located between the cabin and the lounge, is ideal for storing your clothes and belongings. Equipped with a large mirror, it also has a wardrobe and various drawers directly accessible from the shower room, for added practicality. Access to the cargo area is just below the dressing room.

The bedroom

The sleeping area consists of a large XXL drop-down bed 160 x 200 cm. Well thought out, the placement of the TV arm allows you to watch it from the comfort of your bed. A bed in the living room can accommodate 2 additional travelers since the P696U is also equipped for 4 vehicle registration seats.

The storage area

The P720U's atypical configuration features a large side hold, located to the right of the cell door. Lowered to make loading and unloading easier, it is accessible from both inside and outside, under the dressing room. The wicket door is fitted with a compression lock on a double seal, ensuring water and air tightness.

The outside

With its hail-resistant polyester walls and roof, this profile has everything you could wish for. Its robust, durable structure is renowned for its insulating qualities. Accessible hatches make emptying the water easy. The optional underbody drawer gives you 70 kg of extra storage space. Ideal for storing boots or ski boots that are still wet.
