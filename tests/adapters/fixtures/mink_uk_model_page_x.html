MINK-X

FROM £20,995.00

The ultimate off-road, all-terrain teardrop caravan.

Built for trail seekers who aren't afraid to take the road less travelled, the MINK-X has more ground clearance and larger all-season tyres to tackle tougher terrain with confidence.

Off-road ready

If your idea of a good weekend starts where the road ends, the MINK-X is the natural choice. It takes everything people love about the MINK and adds the extra clearance, tyre setup and rugged practicality needed for rougher terrain. The result is a lightweight teardrop caravan that is easier to tow, easier to live with and far more at home off the beaten track than a traditional caravan ever could be.

OPEN-AIR KITCHEN

A teardrop caravan should encourage you to cook outside, eat outside and live outside... but not punish you for it. The MINK-X allows you to do things your way, without compromise.

The rear of the MINK-X opens to an illuminated cooking area with storage and a countertop, plus an integrated ice chest and power points.

RUGGED AESTHETIC

If you're the owner of a big, beefy, well-equipped 4x4, the last thing you want is a dainty little caravan following behind, spoiling the image.

The MINK-X is designed with that in mind, to be the perfect compliment to your all-terrain vehicle. The stylish matte-black surround gives a premium, off-road feeling and looks even better with a bit of dirt on it!

ALL SEASON

The MINK-X is designed for use in all seasons, so your getaways aren't limited to just the summer months - it's not a fairweather camper!

The innovate, frameless Solid Shell Structure is lined with high-thermal insulation, maintaining a steady temperature in the cabin, and the efficient Webasto diesel heating system will keep you toasty warm in even the most frigid of conditions.

NATURE FRIENDLY

The aerodynamic, lightweight design opens up a variety of possibilities for using more environmentally friendly vehicles, including many EVs. Weighing just 520kg, towing the MINK is smooth, comfortable and effortless, making it an excellent choice for beginners. The MINK is also constructed using a number of recycled materials, reducing the carbon footprint considerably.

UNRIVALLED QUALITY

MINK Campers are built to withstand the tests of time; every element has been carefully refined over multiple design rounds, using real-world feedback from "on the ground" testing in Iceland. The highest quality, industry-leading materials are used in every aspect of construction and ensure that your MINK will provide years of fun and enjoyment with little hassle.

Cabin

The MINK-X is small on the outside but spacious on the inside, with a large panoramic skylight and round windows on each side to bring the outside in, with plenty of natural light providing a spacious, roomy feeling. The large, luxurious double bed with memory foam mattress ensures you get a great nights sleep, waking well rested and ready to tackle whatever the day may bring.

Kitchen

The back of the MINK Camper opens up to reveal a well organised and illuminated cooking area, with plenty of storage space. The counter top accommodates a built-in illuminated ice chest. With no fixed appliances and 2 power sockets (12v + 230v) you have the freedom to choose how you want to prepare your meals; use the included gas stove, or, if you prefer, bring your own appliances - induction hobs, air fryers, electric grills or a simple barbeque - the choice is yours.
