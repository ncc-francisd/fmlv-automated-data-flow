Next Generation Ford EcoBlue 170bhp Engine

Powerful, clean and refined, Panama campervans feature Ford’s next generation engine with an automatic gearbox. Featuring Keyless entry and start, Speed sign recognition, Lane-keeping and driver alerts, Wrong way alert, Traction, Torque Vectoring & Trailer Sway Control, Electronic stability control (ESC) and LED headlights with static bending lights.

Integrated cab blinds provide a quick and convenient way to create privacy when you’re parked up, without the need for separate screens.

Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking and Hill start Assist
