How many people can sleep in an Ace campervan?

The Ace campervan 1200 sleeps up to 4 people with the range offering 2 or 4 berth models, allowing you to choose a layout that suits your sleeping requirements.

How do I order an Ace campervan?

Ordering your Ace campervan is a straightforward and personalised process. Simply find your local dealership from our exclusive network and secure your vehicle of choice. From first enquiry to collection, the Ace team is here to ensure a smooth and enjoyable buying experience.

What are the running costs of owning a campervan?

The running costs of owning a campervan can vary depending on how often you travel, the size of your vehicle, and where it’s stored. You’ll need to factor in insurance, MOT, servicing, fuel and campsite fees. However, most owners find the ongoing costs manageable, especially when compared to the flexibility and savings a campervan holiday can offer.

What is the difference between a motorhome and a campervan?

A campervan is typically a smaller, van-based vehicle whilst a motorhome is a larger vehicle and they usually include more amenities and features than a campervan. The choice between a campervan and motorhome ultimately depends on your personal preferences, budget, and travel needs.
