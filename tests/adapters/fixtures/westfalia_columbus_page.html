Westfalia Columbus

The minute you step inside a Westfalia Columbus – you’ll understand why it won MMM Motorhome of the Year Awards 2015. It was named of course after explorer Christopher Columbus; he completed four voyages across the Atlantic, Westfalia bring you four fabulous models honouring his name, giving you the freedom to travel and the reliability that you’d expect from one of the pioneers of camper van conversions.

All models showcase innovative space saving design and up-to-the-minute style; clever use of materials brings a homely, luxurious feel, and the interiors are surprisingly roomy - light and airy by day, snug and cosy at night.

Kitchens are so well thought-out, with space for pots and pans, a two burner hob, good size worktop, and a user-friendly slide out fridge that you can access from inside or outside the vehicle.

The rear sleeping area offers both comfort and privacy and there’s a choice of bed arrangements; the bathroom, though compact isn’t without the room you need, with a large shower tub, wash basin, storage and shelves. Add to that optional leather upholstery and a choice of exterior colours and that’s all the right boxes ticked.

How to Winterise your Westfalia Columbus

Get your campervan prepared before winter with our short video showing you the steps you should take to ensure your Westfalia Columbus is ready for your next camping adventure.

  1. Turn off the switch for the pump underneath the front passenger seat.
  2. Open all the taps and shower into the middle position allowing both hold and cold to drain.
  3. Open the valve on the fresh water tank to start draining it out.
  4. Drain down the waste water tank using the lever underneath the campervan.
  5. Columbus 601D & 641E: Open the two water valves inside the kitchen cupboard.
  6. Columbus 600D & 640E: Remove the bottom drawer and open the two water valves beneath.
  7. Columbus 540D: Open the two water valves underneath the bench seat.
  8. Flush the toilet, drain the septic tank and refill the odour additive.
  9. Recharge the vehicle electricity.
  10. Leave all the taps and drain valves open during winter.
