Built on solid foundations
While nodding stylistically to their iconic heritage,  
the California vehicles will now be built on the 
innovative modular transverse matrix (MQB) platform 
used on the long Multivan. 
This means they will feature the same broad  
range of driver assist systems, from oncoming vehicle 
braking to Travel Assist semi-automated driving 
assistance. 
Longer than its predecessor, the California measures 
5,173 mm in length (previously 4,904 mm), but at the 
same height, it remains suitable for most multi-storey 
car parks.
