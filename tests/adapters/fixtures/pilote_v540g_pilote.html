V540G PILOTE

There's no question of the shortest of Pilote's converted vans sacrificing anything because of its reduced length (5.41m). It's the ideal model for use on any terrain. In town and country.

The V540G in detail

The Pilote V540G converted van offers a spacious and comfortable grand salon that can accommodate up to four people. Its clever layout includes a practical side toilet cabinet, providing convenience and privacy. Additionally, the vehicle's garage bed features a comfortable mattress and a slatted base for a restful sleep. The large storage compartment in the van is accessible from both the interior and exterior, offering increased convenience for luggage and equipment storage. This converted van is perfect for adventurers who value space, comfort, and functionality during their travels.

The lounge

This 5.41 m van has a large dinette area where 4 people can sit comfortably, thanks to the extendable table and the bench seat's great seating depth. USB ports are easily accessible for recharging your electrical appliances.

The kitchen

The kitchen unit is fitted with a sink and 2-burner piezo hob combo, which can be extended using the retractable extension. 3 drawers (with end-of-travel dampers) and an overhead cupboard provide storage for your kitchen equipment.

The bedroom

The V540G, as its name suggests, is equipped with a garage bed with a comfort mattress with high resilience foam and slatted base. The maximum dimensions of the bed are 135 x 194 cm. There are plenty of overhead cupboards on either side of the bed, concealing USB ports. As an option, you can add a larger bedroom cupboard.

The bathroom

The V540G's side shower room guarantees a large shower area. The retractable washbasin and mirror have plenty of storage space.

The garage

Accessible from both inside and outside, the V540G’s garage offers generous storage space under the bed. Ideal for transporting your gear, it combines volume, practicality, and integrated LED lighting.
