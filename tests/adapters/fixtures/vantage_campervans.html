FAQ's

Do you stock extra accessories?

Yes, we do. When you visit our modern manufacturing facility in Leeds, you can also explore our wide range of accessories in our specialist accessory shop.

Do campervans require an MOT?

While new models will not need an MOT for the first three years, after this they will require an annual Class 4 MOT to comply with safety standards.

Do you offer part exchange?

Yes, we do. We offer the opportunity to exchange your current motorhome, caravan or car, no matter the make or model, against a new Vantage model. Contact us today to find out more from a helpful member of our team.

Fuze Campervan

The Vantage Fuze campervan is sure to provide an unforgettable ride. Built on the Ford Transit Custom and coming in at 4.97m, this compact campervan offers effortless manoeuvrability, easy parking and a leisurely driving experience enjoyed by everyone onboard.

In addition to the sleek external design, the Fuze campervan comes with privacy glass installed in the rear for peace of mind.

Notable features include the Reimo Rock and Roll V3000 seating and bed system with multi position floor rails to allow for spacious storage options. Equipped with a 100w solar panel, you can enjoy the extra power and comforts this provides when camping off-grid.

The Fuze campervan also comes with REMIS cab blinds, 12v USB points, a heating and cold water system and more.

Luna Conversion

The Luna campervan from Vantage is designed to offer everything you need to ensure a memorable experience. With a Ford Transit Custom base, this compact campervan comes in at 4.97m, making it easy to park whether on a busy street or a hard to reach getaway.

The Reimo Rock and Roll V3000 seating and bed system offers a multi position seating unit for extra storage and easily converts into a comfortable double bed.

In addition to a pop-top double bed with ladder access, this campervan also includes features such as Webasto diesel heating, an inbuilt solar panel, an underslung gas tank and more. Whether near us in Leeds, Yorkshire or further afield, the Luna campervan can take you where you need to go in both style and comfort.
