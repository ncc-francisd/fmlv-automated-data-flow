Award-Winning Design, Refined Through Experience
RP Innovation and Technology
At RP, innovation isn’t about technology for its own 
sake. It’s about solving problems, improving the 
owner experience and finding better ways to build.
Working closely with Alde, RP became the first 
manufacturer to engineer the Compact 4000 D 
hydronic heating system as a production solution for 
a small panel-van motorhome. For 2027, this quiet, 
even heating system is standard on every Rebel.
RP has also embraced modern waterless sanitation, 
with both the Clesana C1 and OGO composting toilet 
available as standard choices, removing the need for 
a conventional cassette system and giving owners 
greater flexibility away from facilities.
RP Smart Assist brings vehicle information and 
control together through our own interface on an 
included iPad mini, while Victron 24V electrics provide 
a strong foundation for greater battery capacity, 
inverter power and extended off-grid capability.
Alongside these headline innovations are countless 
smaller details — bespoke lightweight panels, 
3D-printed components, custom fibreglass parts and 
solutions developed in-house specifically for the Rebel.
Award-Winning Design — Year After Year
That commitment to thoughtful design continues to 
earn industry recognition.
The Rebel has built an especially strong track record 
of its own. In 2024, the Rebel AWD was recognised at 
the Caravan and Motorhome Club Awards, followed in 
2025 by the Special Edition Rebel Raid AWD — further 
proof that RP’s compact platform can combine 
innovation, comfort and genuine adventure capability.
That success continued across the wider RP range. 
In December 2025, the Rebellion 2 AWD Pro was 
named Best 4x4 Campervan at the Out & About 
Campervan Awards, recognising its design, versatility 
and capability.
Hot on the heels of that success, in February 2026 the 
Rebellion 2 won the Caravan and Motorhome Club 
Award for Any All Wheel Drive Van Conversion over 
6.5 metres.
Together with the Rebellion AWD win in 2023 and 
earlier category successes in 2022 and 2020, these 
awards reflect a consistent approach to refining both 
Rebel and Rebellion around real-world use.
For us, the awards are welcome recognition — but 
the real goal remains the same: to keep refining every 
RP around the people who actually use them.

Alde Comfort Taken Further
The Raid builds on the Rebel’s standard Alde 
Compact 4000 D diesel hydronic heating system 
with several of its most useful upgrades included.
Underfloor heating extends warmth beneath the 
living space, while the engine heat exchanger can 
transfer otherwise wasted engine heat into the Alde 
system while travelling, warming the habitation area 
and water before you arrive.
Electronic drain valves and an Alde AquaClear 
drinking-water system are standard, using UV-C LED 
technology and carbon filtration to neutralise around 
99% of micro-organisms while improving water 
quality and taste.
Special Edition Rebel Raid - £199,995
Our special edition Rebel Raid is designed to hit the 
sweet spot between compact agility, serious off-
grid capability and a specification built for extended 
adventure. At just 5.93m long, the Rebel’s medium-
wheelbase platform remains remarkably easy to 
manoeuvre, while Rebel Raid adds many of the 
systems, equipment and exterior upgrades owners 
would otherwise select individually.
Rather than simply adding cosmetic detail, Rebel 
Raid substantially increases the Rebel’s capability. 
Electrical power, heating, connectivity, comfort and 
exterior equipment are all upgraded as part of the 
standard package, creating a compact motorhome 
ready for longer stays away from conventional site 
infrastructure.
Enhanced Electrical Freedom
At the heart of Rebel Raid is a substantially upgraded 
24V Victron electrical system. A 300A 24V battery 
provides three times the Rebel’s standard battery 
capacity, supported by dual XS1400 chargers and a 
powerful Victron Quattro 5000VA inverter/charger.
The Quattro converts stored battery energy into 
a familiar 240V mains supply, allowing suitable 
domestic electrical equipment to be used without 
an external hook-up. More importantly, Rebel Raid 
adds an engine-driven alternator/generator capable 
of producing up to 5kW while the engine is running.

Robust Connections
The Raid comes with RP’s Premium Mobile Wi-Fi 
system as standard, using its own SIM and externally 
mounted antenna to improve the chances of finding 
and maintaining a usable mobile connection.
A dedicated router then creates a permanent Wi-Fi 
network inside the vehicle for phones, tablets, 
laptops and the included Avtex Smart TV.
The Raid also includes a dedicated Starlink Mini 
power connection. For owners using their own 
Starlink hardware, satellite connectivity provides 
another route online when travelling beyond reliable 
4G or 5G coverage.
Note: Starlink Mini hardware and subscription are not included.
Comfort Built In
Several of the Rebel’s most popular upgrades are 
included as standard on Rebel Raid. Remis cab 
blinds provide quick privacy when parked, while the 
side-door flyscreen allows ventilation without inviting 
insects inside. At the rear, the U-shaped lounge 
includes an 18.5” Avtex HD Smart TV with Maxview 
Gazelle Pro booster, Maxxfan Deluxe roof fan/light 
and locking safe. For owners touring regularly 
in hotter climates, the Maxxfan can instead be 
upgraded to Dometic 3000W roof-mounted AC.
Upgraded Interior Finish
Exclusive half-leather upholstery throughout is 
standard on Rebel Raid, combining the warmth of 
fabric with durable leather where it is most useful.
Full or two-tone Exclusive leather upgrades are 
available, together with bespoke worktop colours 
and Karndean flooring for owners wanting to 
personalise the interior further.
The kitchen also gains a Grohe black pull-out swan-
neck tap as standard.
Built for Outdoor Living
Rebel Raid’s exterior specification is considerably 
more comprehensive than the Rebel’s.
A 3.5m Thule manual awning with LED strip lighting 
creates useful shelter outside the vehicle, while the 
electric side step improves access. The removable 
interior table can also be used outside with the 
included tripod and leg, and an external 240V socket 
provides convenient power alongside the vehicle.

Forge Steel Adventure Storage
Rebel Raid also includes a Forge Steel exterior 
equipment package comprising tyre carrier, safari 
carrier and storage box.
Moving bulky or dirty equipment outside the 
habitation area preserves valuable interior storage 
and provides a robust platform for adventure 
equipment better carried externally. Further bespoke 
Forge Steel solutions can also be specified.
Distinctive Raid Exterior
Exclusive Rebel Raid graphics distinguish the 
model immediately, accompanied by front Lazer 
grille lights, RP’s 6m side-skirt body kit and a Black 
Raptor-finished Mercedes badge.
Owners who prefer a subtler appearance can delete 
the Rebel Raid graphics at no cost, while further 
Black Raptor treatments, additional Lazer lighting 
and a roof-rack and ladder system are available.
Ready for More Demanding Travel
The Rebel Raid’s compact proportions and available 
Mercedes-Benz AWD platform already provide an 
exceptionally capable base for more adventurous 
touring.
For owners venturing further off the beaten track, 
an optional under-bonnet ARB compressor provides 
a convenient way to adjust tyre pressures to suit 
changing terrain, while optional sand tracks with 
magnetic mounts add useful recovery equipment 
without consuming valuable interior space.
A Focused Specification
The idea behind Rebel Raid is not to provide an 
endless list of choices. It is to start with a carefully 
selected specification that already includes many 
of the upgrades owners choose when creating a 
serious touring Rebel.
The key systems for electrical independence, 
heating, connectivity, entertainment, comfort and 
outdoor living are already in place. What remains is 
largely about personalising the finish and adding the 
specialist equipment that suits the way you intend to 
travel.
The Rebel Raid takes the agility and intelligent 
packaging of the Rebel and gives it a very different 
level of built-in capability — a compact home-from-
home designed to keep going when the site, hook-
up and conventional infrastructure are left behind.
