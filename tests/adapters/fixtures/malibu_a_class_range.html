The A-Class motorhome that is setting new standards.

At Malibu, we’re only satisfied with the standard if we’re the ones setting it ourselves. And that’s exactly what we’re doing with the new generation of A-Class Malibu motorhomes. The brand-new model range is available on either the Fiat Ducato or Mercedes-Benz Sprinter chassis, as a lightweight 3.5 t or comfort 4.2 t model, and boasts a striking new design inside and out.

On the outside, the new front mask with its sleek radiator grille and Malibu badging, as well as the new Dynamic Design rear end and the new exterior decals ensure that the A-Class motorhome makes a lasting impression from front to back. Inside, the two new style ranges – nomos and chiaro – give the interior a contemporary look that flows throughout the motorhome. And talking of flow – comfort and a true sense of well-being can be felt in every last inch of the motorhome.

Impressively lightweight.

The innovative lightweight construction of our lightweight models ensures that the vehicles stay within the lightweight 3.5 t limit without having to sacrifice on comfort or ample payload reserves. The perfect class for drivers with a category B driving licence – even as a 4-person vehicle.

Comfort without limits.

Our 4.2 t comfort models are the perfect choice for those who are not dependent on the 3.5 t weight class and want to experience premium comfort. The huge payload reserves of these fully-equipped motorhomes leave nothing to be desired.

Unique double floor with the largest storage compartment in its class.

You can never have enough storage space on holiday. That’s why all Malibu A-Class motorhomes provide lots of room, thanks to the extra-large double floor storage space and many easily accessible storage compartments, ensuring that your holiday luggage is stowed away safely and neatly in an instant. Even bulky items of luggage such as camping furniture or skis can be quickly and easily stowed away thanks to the central through-loading storage compartment. And thanks to the LED lighting, you will always be able to see all of your luggage. The numerous large exterior hatches ensure that it is easy to load the vehicle from outside, while the large, self-supporting floor hatches in the living and kitchen area of the motorhome provide convenient access to the extra-deep, recessed storage compartments from the inside. The double floor storage space also acts as a heat store to protect your cargo from frost, which is handy when it gets colder.

As an example Malibu I 470 RB-LE “K” comfort 4.2 t

Large, heated double floor storage compartment (usable height up to 55,5 cm) with central through-loading area (interior height 22 cm)

Large living area floor hatch in the kitchen area

Huge living area floor hatch in entrance area, also easy to load from the outside when the XL habitation door is open, second living area floor hatch in kitchen area (usable depth of up to 46 cm)

Convenient loading of double floor storage compartment at passenger side via another large external hatch. Interior access via L-shaped lounge seating area, lift-up side bench seat (not I 441 KB-LE), as well as as via large living area floor hatch in entrance and kitchen area
