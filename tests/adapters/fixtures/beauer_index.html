Who Beauer are and their French heritage

Beauer is Europe’s foremost creator of innovative mobile and expandable spaces for living and working.

Headquartered in Cholet, France, the company has seen increasing recognition since its founding, earning attention from customers across Europe and around the globe.

Driven by a commitment to innovation and precision engineering, Beauer continues to develop pioneering solutions that build upon its specialist knowledge of expansion systems and interlocking mechanisms.

How the expanding caravan concept works

A Living Space That Travels and Expands with You

More than just a caravan, every Beauer model is designed to transform your travel experience with a unique expandable living space that adapts to your lifestyle. Whether you’re exploring as a couple or setting off on a family adventure, the intelligently designed interior maximises every centimetre for exceptional comfort. Enjoy the freedom to travel without compromise, wherever the road takes you.

From Compact to Spacious in Moments

Setting up your living space at the campsite is quick, simple and effortless.

At the heart of every Beauer is a patented telescopic expansion system that allows two integrated modules to extend horizontally, increasing the available living area to up to three times its transport size.

When closed, the furniture is cleverly nested within the structure to optimise space. As the unit expands, each element automatically moves into its intended position, creating a fully functional living environment without any manual rearrangement.

No complicated setup is required. Simply press the activation button located at the rear, and the electric cylinders complete the expansion process in around 60 seconds.

When folded for transport, the exterior dimensions remain comparable to those of a conventional vehicle, making towing easier, improving manoeuvrability, and often giving the impression that you’re hardly towing a caravan at all.

The benefits of the design compared to a traditional caravan

Why Choose a Beauer Over a Traditional Caravan?

Compact Dimensions, Effortless Towing

One of the biggest advantages of a Beauer is its remarkably compact size when closed. Measuring less than 2 metres wide and under 4 metres long, it is easy to tow, manoeuvre and position, even for drivers with limited caravan experience. Its reduced footprint also makes storage far simpler compared to larger motorhomes or recreational vehicles, while still providing up to 12 square metres of living space when expanded.

Fast, Hassle-Free Setup

Another key benefit is the speed and simplicity of deployment. Thanks to Beauer's electrically powered expansion system, the living area can be transformed in under a minute with minimal effort. There are no complex mechanisms to unfold, no stabilising structures to assemble, and no furniture to move into place manually. Everything is integrated into a single, ready-to-use solution designed for convenience.

Enhanced Comfort and Everyday Living

Once fully expanded, a Beauer offers a bright, practical and thoughtfully designed interior that makes the most of every available centimetre. The layout has been carefully engineered to create an impressive sense of space, with clearly defined areas for sleeping, dining and cooking. Effective insulation also allows for comfortable year-round use, providing a welcoming environment across all seasons.

Exceptional Versatility

A Beauer is much more than a holiday caravan. Its adaptable design allows it to be used in a wide range of settings, from guest accommodation and glamping rentals to promotional suites, hospitality spaces and mobile business units. This flexibility makes it an attractive option for both leisure users and commercial operators alike.

A Smarter Environmental Choice

Beauer's innovative design can also help reduce the environmental impact associated with travel and accommodation. Because it is towed by a separate vehicle, owners can choose lighter and more efficient tow vehicles, including electric models where suitable. Combined with French manufacturing standards, a long operational lifespan, and the potential for refurbishment and reuse, Beauer offers a more sustainable long-term approach to mobile living.
