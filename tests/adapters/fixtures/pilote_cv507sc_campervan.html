Campervans

CV507SC PILOTE CAMPERVAN

Up to 7 seats onboard! The CV507SC is a unique proposition in the world of campervans. Truly a two-in-one vehicle, it can take on daily travel and escapades alike. Thanks to its modular layout, it easily adapts to any use, in car mode, in travel mode, without compromising comfort or function.

The CV507SC in detail

Your second car, and so much more With its 7 seats, the CV507SC is ideal for everyday use as a true family car. School runs, commuting, shopping or family outings: it can easily replace a second car. When the weekend comes, its versatile layout transforms your everyday vehicle into the perfect companion for getting away.

Life on board

With its many configuration options, the CV507SC adapts to every moment of the day. Enjoy a versatile interior space designed to accommodate up to 7 people and easily switch from everyday use to family getaways.

Storage

The CV507SC features two storage compartments integrated into the furniture on the driver’s side, making it easy to keep your belongings within reach. In the 4-seat configuration, it also offers a large rear storage area, ideal for easily carrying your luggage and leisure equipment.

Sleeping

The CV507SC comes as standard with a pop-up roof, offering a comfortable sleeping area upstairs. For even greater versatility, a removable bed is also available as an option at the rear of the vehicle. It unfolds very easily, quickly transforming the space into a proper bedroom.

Kitchen

Located at the rear of the vehicle, the CV507SC’s kitchen features a gas hob, making it easy to prepare meals during your getaways. A sink unit is also available as an option, providing even more comfort and practicality on board.
