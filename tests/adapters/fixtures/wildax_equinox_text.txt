New for 2026 season, built on the iconic Ford Transit chassis, the Equinox is a versatile 4 berth motorhome offering a rear double bed and large double in the elevating roof. An optional upgrade to 165BHP is also available for that little bit more ‘go’! As standard the Equinox is supplied on the 6-speed manual gearbox, but also available is the fantastic 8-speed automatic option for a more relaxed drive! We also have the option for an all-wheel driving experience, found within the 4×4 model variant.

The superb Ford Transit 130 BHP is used as the base for the Equinox. Seen on UK roads since 1965, the Ford Transit is a favourite for commercial and now campervan use! The Equinox is very car like in its interior design and driving style. This high spec model has all the features you would expect to see in a luxury motor – such as large touch screen, digital dash screen, electric parking brake, parking camera and sensors, cruise control and much more!

Lifestyle highlights
It's all in the details
This range is packed with the latest tech, featuring a 100Ahr lithium leisure battery and a 200W solar panel as standard, making it perfect for camping off the grid. The only gas used is for the 2-burner hob and oven in the kitchen, powered by a 6kg gas bottle stored in the gas locker.
The Equinox 4×4 all-wheel drive version has a slightly higher internal floor but externally dimensions remain the same.
Fully winterised 100 litre fresh and 51 litre waste water tanks.
Drive highlights
Built on 130BHP engine as standard, but can upgrade to 165BHP.
Both models come with a 6-speed manual gearbox as standard, but if you prefer a smoother ride there’s also a great 8-speed automatic upgrade.
DAB radio with USB/aux socket
Phone projection for sat nav (Android/Apple)
Cruise control, cab air conditioning, reversing camera and sensors
Alloy wheels
