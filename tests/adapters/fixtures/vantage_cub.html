CUB

5.41m (17'9")

Click Here to Spec Up Your CUB
2 Berth
2 Travel Seats

We have designed the CUB van conversion for those who are seeking a very driveable 5.41m motorhome that is easy to park and has the option of two single beds or one large super king size bed.

Its comprehensive range of features makes it the ideal upgrade from a pop-top or a convenient option for those looking to downsize. As well as a full oven/grill with a three burner hob, the sink and low energy fridge freezer provide a luxury experience that everyone onboard can enjoy. With a sizeable lounge, fully accessorised washroom and detailed kitchen area, this all-inclusive yet compact van conversion has everything you need for a carefree getaway.

Take a Closer Look

KITCHEN AREA

The Kitchen area is located directly behind the passenger seat. 

The full oven/grill, three burner hob and low energy fridge freezer are perfect for a motorhome of this size. There is also a handy storage cupboard for cutlery and crockery. Worktops are finished in a durable scratch-resistant laminate. (induction hob available)

WRAP AROUND LOUNGE

The Sizeable lounge gives the key living area the feeling of space
This space quickly converts into either 2 singe beds or one super king size bed
Storage is plentiful with large under-bed boxes and overhead locker storage

WASHROOM

The washroom incorporates a shower, toilet, large sink and spacious bathroom cabinet which gives you everything you need for off-grid camping.

BLINDS 

Throughout the vehicle we fit Remis blinds including the cab. Visually impressive with its surrounding frame and pleated material to help you get a better nights sleep. This prevents others from looking in and protects the cab from sunlight.
SEATS IN A CUB CANNOT BE ROTATED

USB / USB-C 

The lounge area is equipped with USB/ USB-C points either side for easy access

WARDROBE SPACE 

The CUB offers large wardrobe storage space and a hanging rail for clothes.
