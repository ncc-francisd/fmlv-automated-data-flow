The MRO is is the mass of the unladen vehicle, including an allowance for the driver (assumed 75 kg) and a 90% full fuel tank. The MRO is calculated with the fresh water tank empty. If you travel with water in the freshwater tank then the User Payload will reduce accordingly.

The optional automatic gearbox on the Fiat chassis will increase the MRO by 50 kg and therefore reducing the user payload by the same amount. All masses and weights are subject to a tolerance of +/-5% complying with the European Directive 1230/2012. All motorhomes in this brochure conform to the European Standards for Motorhomes: EN1646 -1, EN 721, EN1949 and EN 1648-2. They have also been granted European Whole Vehicle Type Approval to EC Directive 2007/46. We would strongly advise against modifying vehicles in any way that could affect the validity of these Standards or Directives.

The Maximum User Payload includes:

Conventional load, is the allowance for passengers.

Essential habitation equipment includes items and/or fluids required for safe and proper functioning of the habitation equipment.Optional equipment is items available from the manufacturer over and above the standard specification.

Personal effects, items not effected by the above.

Made for maximum lightness, strength and ease of assembly

The pop-top roof fitted to the Auto-Sleeper Active range is engineered from high-quality fiberglass and fully insulated with 2 cm of polyurethane to maintain comfort throughout the seasons.

Designed with the needs of Active owners in mind, the lightweight construction helps maximise payload while ensuring excellent structural strength. Careful weight distribution and a modern fiberglass lamination process contribute to the roof’s exceptionally low mass, enhancing overall vehicle efficiency and drivability.

The advanced Sky Up lifting system, featuring smooth double-acting operation, makes raising and lowering the roof effortless. Whether you're setting up camp or packing away, the mechanism provides a secure, stable experience—performing reliably even in challenging weather conditions such as strong winds or unexpected snowfall.
