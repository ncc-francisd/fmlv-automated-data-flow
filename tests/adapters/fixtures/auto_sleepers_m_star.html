Designed for those who seek the finest in travel, this campervan offers unparalleled comfort and sophistication on the road.

The MRO is is the mass of the unladen vehicle, including an allowance for the driver (assumed 75 kg) and a 90% full fuel tank. The MRO is calculated with the fresh water tank empty. If you travel with water in the freshwater tank then the User Payload will reduce accordingly. 

 All masses and weights are subject to a tolerance of +/-5% complying with the European Directive 1230/2012. All motorhomes in this brochure conform to the European Standards for Motorhomes: EN1646 -1, EN 721, EN1949 and EN 1648-2. They have also been granted European Whole Vehicle Type Approval to EC Directive 2007/46. We would strongly advise against modifying vehicles in any way that could affect the validity of these Standards or Directives.

The Maximum User Payload includes: 
Conventional load, is the allowance for passengers
Essential habitation equipment includes items and/or fluids required for safe and proper functioning of the habitation equipment.Optional equipment is items available from the manufacturer over and above the standard specification.
Personal effects, items not effected by the above.

Discover Your Home Comforts at a Glance

Explore the standout internal features of your Auto-Sleepers M-Star campervan. Fuel your adventures in a fully equipped kitchen, Dream Big to Explore more after a restful nights sleep, and refresh on the go with a multifunctional washroom.

Three Burner Hob

Your M-Star kitchen includes a flexible 3 burner hob with separate oven and grill.

Microwave Oven

All M-star Campervans feature a 230v Dometic microwave oven for convenient cooking on tour.

Fridge-Freezer

Keep your food fresh with the large compressor refrigerator with Removable Ice Box and Dual Hinged Door

LED Interior Lighting

Create the right interior atmosphere with bright, energy effcient LED lights. Including easily positionable reading spotlights.

Durable Upholstery 

Our fabrics have passed the Martindale rub test over 100,000 making them more durable with enhanced longevity.

Find Out More >

Mirror Mirror

A sleek, full-length mirror adds depth and practicality to the M-Star interior.

What Dreams are Made of

Retiring to bed couldn’t be simpler. Slide out the bedframe and premium quality Reflex™ foam cushions to double up as a comfortable mattress. The M-Star campervan offers a cozy and inviting bedroom area. The rear lounge converts to sleeping quarters featuring a comfortable double bed or two singles for a restful night’s sleep.

Spacious Storage Facilities

To help make the most of your space, our designers have included numerous space saving storage options to securely stow your luggage.

Impeccable Washroom Luxury

The M-Star Campervan comes with a well-appointed bathroom that includes a toilet, sink, and shower. The bathroom is designed for efficiency and convenience, allowing you to freshen up and maintain your hygiene while on the road.

Dual USB-A and USB-C Outlets: Stay Powered Anywhere

Easily charge your devices using the onboard 5 x Dual USB-A and USB-C Outlets. USB-A ports allow campers to charge their older devices, while the versatile USB-C ports accommodate newer gadgets, offering faster charging capabilities.

Dine Al’Fresco!

Feel like making the most of some glorious weather? All Auto-Sleeper models feature external BBQ points for quick and easy outdoor dining.

Truma Aventa Comfort

Enjoy a pleasant indoor climate thanks to the Truma Aventa Comfort Plus roof-mounted air conditioning system.
