Small towing vehicles

Ideal for camping beginners: Thanks to its compact dimensions and low total weight, the ERIBA Touring is also ideal for smaller towing vehicles and holders of a standard car driving license.

Low fuel consumption

The aerodynamic front, consisting of the streamlined tubular steel construction, ensures low air resistance and thus reduced fuel consumption.

Interior climate

The pop-top roof provides fresh air in the caravan and thus creates the perfect room climate thanks to the pop-top roof window, which is made of canvas material.

Individuality

Free rein for your personality: Different exterior colours, various interior designs and matching fabric combinations as well as sophisticated accessories as original accessories ensure that your caravan is really your caravan.

Driving safety

The unique design principle is from aircraft construction, consisting of a robust steel tube cage with aluminum casing, and the compact vehicle dimensions offer perfect tracking characteristics and therefore outstanding driving safety.

Storage space

Despite its compact dimensions, the Touring offers plenty of storage space thanks to all-round roof storage cabinets and intelligent space solutions.

The aerodynamic design.

With its aerodynamic bow and rounded edges, the Touring is a model of efficiency and timeless elegance.
