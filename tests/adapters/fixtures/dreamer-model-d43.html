The advantages of the converted van: D43

The +

    Compact, comfortable and practical: a family campervan which adapts to your needs.

  • A level of flexibility that sets no limits on your choice of destinations.
  • A comfortable lounge and dining area where you can make the most of every moment.
  • A permanent layout: beds and washroom always ready for use, no conversion required.
  • A large bed at the rear (1950 x 1320 / 1080 mm) for comfortable nights just like at home.
