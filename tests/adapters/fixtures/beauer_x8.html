Beauer X8

The Ultimate Expandable Living Experience

Designed to accommodate up to eight people, the Beauer X8 delivers the comfort and practicality of a mobile home while retaining the flexibility of a touring caravan.

A mobile home with fully equipped, cozy living spaces.

Spacious Living Without Compromise

The Beauer X8 has been engineered to maximise interior space while maintaining the convenience of a mobile unit. Once expanded, it offers dedicated living, sleeping, cooking and bathroom areas, creating a comfortable base wherever your travels take you.

  • Sleeps up to 8 people comfortably
  • Two separate bedrooms
  • 2 separate bedrooms
  • Dual lounge areas with additional sleeping capacity
  • Fully equipped kitchen with extensive storage
  • Spacious bathroom facilities
  • Rapid electric expansion system for easy setup

A Lounge Designed for Family and Friends

The spacious living area has been created as a welcoming social hub, ideal for dining, relaxing and spending time together. A comfortable sofa bed and a large dining area provide seating for up to eight people, making it perfect for family holidays and group adventures.

Large windows allow natural light to fill the interior, while integrated flyscreens and blackout blinds offer privacy and comfort whenever needed. High-quality insulated wall construction also helps maintain a pleasant indoor environment throughout the seasons.

A Kitchen Built for Extended Stays

The X8 features a generously equipped kitchen designed to make everyday living on the road both practical and enjoyable. A large refrigerator, sink and choice of cooking appliances provide everything needed to prepare meals with ease.

Thoughtfully integrated storage solutions ensure there is ample room for food, cookware and household essentials, while dedicated space is available for optional appliances such as an oven or dishwasher.

An additional benefit of the expandable structure is that when the unit is folded for transport, the interior remains securely enclosed and protected from view.

Comfortable Sleeping Accommodation

The Beauer X8 offers two separate bedrooms, each featuring a spacious double bed designed for maximum comfort. High-quality mattresses and a practical layout help create a relaxing environment that feels more like home than a conventional caravan.

A walk-in wardrobe positioned between the bedrooms provides convenient storage and enhanced privacy between sleeping areas. For larger groups, the lounge seating can also be converted into additional beds, allowing accommodation for up to eight occupants.

Spacious Bathroom Facilities

The bathroom has been designed with both comfort and practicality in mind. It includes a cassette toilet, shower enclosure, vanity unit, wash basin and mirror, providing the convenience of a well-appointed washroom wherever you travel.

Additional storage and provision for optional appliances help make the X8 particularly well suited to longer stays and touring holidays.

Expands in Around One Minute

At the heart of the X8 is Beauer's patented telescopic expansion technology. Two integrated sections extend horizontally to dramatically increase the available interior space, creating a living area that is approximately three times larger than its transport configuration.

The process is completely automated. With a simple press of a button, electric actuators extend the structure while the furniture automatically moves into position, eliminating the need for lengthy setup procedures.
